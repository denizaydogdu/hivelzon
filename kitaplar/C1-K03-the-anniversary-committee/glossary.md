# Sözlük: The Anniversary Committee (C1-K03)

C1 dilbilgisi yapıları (devrik yapı, cleft, karma koşul, ortaç yan cümleleri, modal incelikleri, kişisiz edilgen, gerçek dışı geçmiş, eksiltme ve yerine koyma), kurumsal dilin klişeleri, deyimler ve Türk öğrenci notları. Taslak; kullanıcı onayı bekliyor (H-028).

| Kelime | Türkçe | Örnek | Not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not until he said | ancak o … söyleyince | Not until he said the word "volunteers" did everyone else realise theirs. | Devrik yapı (G-C1-01): Not until + yan cümle, ardından yardımcı fiil + özne: did everyone realise. **Dikkat:** ~~Not until he said everyone realised~~ değil. |
| Never once had she spoken | bir kez olsun konuşmamıştı | Never once had she spoken at the summer party. | Devrik yapı: olumsuz zarf (Never) başa gelince had + özne + V3. Düz hâli: She had never once spoken. |
| Not only had she numbered | numaralandırmakla kalmamıştı | Not only had she numbered every item, but she had also estimated how long each one would take. | Devrik yapı: Not only + yardımcı fiil + özne …, but … also. |
| Rarely had Gwen felt | Gwen nadiren hissetmişti | Rarely had Gwen felt anything about an object in a cardboard box beyond a mild interest in its value. | Devrik yapı: Rarely / Seldom başa gelince soru düzeni kullanılır. |
| Only then did she see | ancak o zaman gördü | Only then did she see the problem. | Devrik yapı: Only then + did + özne + yalın fiil. **Dikkat:** ~~Only then she saw~~ değil. |
| No sooner had Gwen opened | Gwen … açar açmaz | No sooner had Gwen opened her mouth to object than the door swung open and Howard came in, as if he had been waiting outside for his cue. | Devrik yapı: No sooner had + özne + V3 … than … (~~when~~ değil; Hardly … when ile karıştırılmaz). |
| Never in my life had I seen | hayatımda hiç görmemiştim | Never in my life had I seen so much black cotton. | Devrik yapı, konuşma dilinde vurgulu anlatım. |
| It was only when | ancak … -diğinde | It was only when Howard Brindle, the managing director, stood up at the front of the boardroom and said the word "celebration" that she realised her mistake. | Cleft yapı (G-C1-02): It was only when … that … zamanı vurgular. |
| It was Nell who | … -en Nell'di | It was Nell who found the first umbrella. | Cleft yapı: It was + vurgulanan kişi + who … Türkçede "İlk şemsiyeyi bulan Nell oldu." |
| What she needed was | ona gereken şey … idi | What she needed was proof, real proof, the kind that not even Howard could laugh away. | Wh-cleft: What + özne + fiil + was … Vurgu cümlenin sonundaki bilgidedir. |
| What Gwen wanted | Gwen'in istediği şey | What Gwen wanted, more than anything, was to put the booklet back in the box and say nothing at all. | Wh-cleft; was'tan sonra to + fiil gelir. |
| If she had picked up | … alsaydı (şimdi … olurdu) | If she had picked up her pen a little faster on that first morning, she thought, she would now be sitting peacefully behind her plant, with the accounts finished and her reputation for invisibility intact. | Karma koşul (G-C1-03): geçmişteki koşul (had + V3) → şimdiki sonuç (would now be + -ing). |
| If you hadn't gone down | sen inmeseydin | If you hadn't gone down to that basement, Gwen, we'd all be celebrating the wrong birthday right now! | Karma koşul: geçmiş koşul, şimdiki sonuç (right now). |
| Had she been | olsaydı | Had she been the kind of person who could do that, her life would have been considerably simpler. | If'siz, devrik koşul: Had she been = If she had been. |
| Having seen your spreadsheets | tablolarını gördükten sonra | "Having seen your spreadsheets, Gwen, I honestly can't think of anyone better. | Ortaç yan cümlesi (G-C1-04): Having + V3 = -dikten sonra; öznesi ana cümleyle aynıdır (I). |
| Having made the tea | çayı yaptıktan sonra | Having made the tea, in a kettle that was older than both of his visitors, Mr Penrose explained the system. | Ortaç yan cümlesi; önce çay yapıldı, sonra sistem anlatıldı. |
| Having spent three evenings | üç akşam geçirdikten sonra | Having spent three evenings in the basement going through the personnel files, she had a list of three hundred and twelve names and addresses, some of them very old, some of them almost certainly out of date, and one of them, as far as she could tell, belonging to a dog that had been employed in 1989 as a security measure. | Ortaç yan cümlesi + spend time doing (zaman geçirmek). **Dikkat:** ~~spent three evenings to go through~~ değil. |
| You needn't have hurried | acele etmenize gerek yoktu | "You needn't have hurried, mind you. | Modal incelik (G-C1-05): needn't have + V3 = yapıldı ama gerek yoktu. Karşılaştır: didn't need to = gerek yoktu (yapılmış olması şart değil). |
| might well have been | pekâlâ … olabilirdi | She kept a plant on her desk that needed so little attention that it might well have been plastic; she had never checked. | Modal incelik: geçmişe dair güçlü olasılık. |
| I'm supposed to be closing | kapatıyor olmam gerekiyor | I'm supposed to be closing the accounts." | be supposed to = beklenen, planlanan görev. Geçmişte: It was supposed to be thirty. |
| I'd rather not | istemem, tercih etmem | "I'd rather not, if it's all the same to everyone," said Gwen. | would rather + yalın fiil; burada eksiltme (not'tan sonra fiil yok). **Dikkat:** ~~I'd rather to not~~ değil. |
| Would you rather I didn't ask | sormasam mı daha iyiydi | "Would you rather I didn't ask?" | Gerçek dışı geçmiş (G-C1-07): would rather + özne + geçmiş zaman = şimdiki zamana dair dilek. |
| It was high time | artık zamanı gelmişti | It was high time, she thought, that somebody at Brindle Umbrellas actually finished a conversation. | Gerçek dışı geçmiş: It's (high) time + özne + geçmiş zaman (finished). |
| as though it were a sword | sanki bir kılıçmış gibi | He was the grandson of the founder, Albert Brindle, whose portrait hung in reception: a small, fierce man holding a closed umbrella as though it were a sword. | as if / as though + were: gerçek olmayan karşılaştırma. |
| He was said to have smiled | gülümsediği söylenirdi | He was said to have smiled twice in thirty-one years, both times by accident. | Kişisiz edilgen (G-C1-06): özne + be said to have + V3 (geçmişe dair). |
| It is often said that | sıkça söylenir ki | It is often said that every building has one great weakness. | Kişisiz edilgen: It is said that … |
| He's believed to have used | kullandığına inanılıyor | "He's believed to have used a cheque as a bookmark for three months once. | Kişisiz edilgen: be believed to have + V3. |
| It's understood that | anlaşılan, bilindiği üzere | It's understood that Mr Brindle wants to make the decision himself." | Kurumsal dilde kişisiz edilgen: kimin söylediği belirtilmez. |
| So did the insurance people | sigortacılar da öyle yaptı | So did the insurance people." | Yerine koyma (G-C1-08): So + yardımcı fiil + özne = … da öyle. |
| So does Mr Brindle | Bay Brindle da öyle | "So does Mr Brindle," said Nell, and they all thought of Clive's logo. | Yerine koyma: önceki cümle present simple olduğu için does. |
| I think so | sanırım öyle | "I'd have to check the minutes," said Nell, "but I think so." | so, bütün bir cümlenin yerini tutar. Olumsuzu: I hope not / I don't think so. **Dikkat:** ~~I think it~~ değil. |
| Mistakes were made | hatalar yapıldı | "Mistakes were made." | Kaçamak edilgen: işi yapan söylenmez. Kurumsal dilin klasik kalıbı. |
| It would appear that | görünüşe bakılırsa | "It would appear that we are a little early. | Temkinli dil: kesin konuşmaktan kaçınır. Benzeri: It would seem that. |
| arguably | tartışmaya açık ama, belki de | "Which is arguably on brand," said Jasper. | Temkinli zarf: iddianın tartışılabileceğini kabul eder. |
| Going forward | bundan sonrası için | "Going forward," said Clive carefully, "I think it's essential that the chair be someone with real bandwidth. | Kurumsal klişe. it's essential that the chair be: resmî dilek kipi (yalın be). |
| circle back | (konuya) yeniden dönmek | "Then let's circle back to it," said Clive, picking up his mug and backing away, "once the logo's been signed off." | Kurumsal klişe; çoğu zaman konuyu ertelemek demektir. |
| take that offline | toplantı dışında konuşmak | Let's take that offline." | Kurumsal klişe. Mizah: "We are offline, Clive. We're in the kitchen." |
| get bogged down in | -e takılıp kalmak | "Let's not get bogged down in numbers, Gwen," said Howard kindly. | Deyim: bog = bataklık; ayrıntıya saplanıp ilerleyememek. |
| with bells on | seve seve, hevesle | I shall be there with bells on, as long as my son can drive me. | Deyim (İngiliz günlük dili): bir davete büyük bir istekle gelmek. |
| like a sieve | kalbur gibi | But my son says my memory's like a sieve, so I wouldn't put any money on it." | Deyim: a memory like a sieve = çok unutkan. I wouldn't put any money on it = buna para yatırmazdım (emin değilim). |
| through no fault of her own | hiçbir kusuru olmadan | And now, through no fault of her own, she was standing in the very centre of it, holding a fifty-metre umbrella. | Eşdizim: kendi suçu olmadan. |
| in living memory | kimsenin hatırlayabildiği kadarıyla | Mr Penrose, at the back, took off his woollen hat for the first time in living memory. | Eşdizim: yaşayanların hatırladığı süre içinde. |
| doing the maths | hesabını yapmak | It had been founded forty-nine years ago, in the same week as Howard, exactly as Howard had been telling everyone for decades without ever once doing the maths. | İngiliz İngilizcesi: maths (Amerikan: math). do the maths = basit hesabı yapmak. |
| since 1976 | 1976'dan beri | It said what it had said for as long as anyone could remember: that Brindle Umbrellas had been keeping the town of Wexley Bridge dry since 1976. | **Dikkat:** since + başlangıç noktası (since 1976), for + süre (for fifty years). ~~from 1976~~ ya da ~~for 1976~~ değil; perfect zamanla kullanılır. |
