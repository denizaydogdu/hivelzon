# Sözlük: Eleven Bridges (C1-K02)

İleri kelimeler, deyimler ve eşdizimler; C1 dilbilgisi yapıları (devrik yapı, cleft, karma koşul, ortaç yan cümleleri, modal incelikleri, kişisiz edilgen, gerçek dışı geçmiş, eksiltme) ve Türk öğrenci notları. Taslak; kullanıcı onayı bekliyor (H-028).

| Kelime | Türkçe | Örnek | Not |
|---|---|---|---|
| loft | tavan arası | I found it on a wet Sunday in June, in my mother's loft, inside a shoebox held together with brown tape. | İngiliz İngilizcesinde yaygın; attic de denir. |
| none of your business | seni ilgilendirmez | Under the diary lay a pair of cycling gloves gone stiff with age, a folded map of the River Ostrel so old that the creases had worn into holes, and a long envelope which I didn't open, because some things in other people's boxes are none of your business. | Deyim. Burada genel "your" (insan) anlamında; kaba değil, kuru bir espri. |
| crumbling | ufalanan, dökülen | A stone arch with every crumbling block shaded separately. | Betimleyici eşdizim: crumbling stone / walls / plaster. crumble = ufalanmak. |
| gushing over the weir | bentten coşkuyla dökülerek | Beneath it the river ran fast and brown after a week of rain, gushing over the weir a little way downstream. | Eşdizim: a gushing river / stream. weir = nehirdeki alçak set. |
| a civil engineer | bir inşaat mühendisi | I should explain that I am a civil engineer. | **Dikkat:** meslek söylerken a / an gerekir: ~~I am civil engineer~~ yanlış. |
| spans | köprü açıklıkları | It was a long, low bridge with three spans, resting on two slender piers, and it was beautiful in the slightly unreal way that buildings in architects' brochures are beautiful. | Teknik dil: span = iki ayak arasındaki açıklık; pier = ara ayak; abutment = kenar ayağı; deck = tabliye. |
| piers | köprü ayakları | The two piers stood precisely where the gravel Bank rose out of the river, with their foundations drawn going down through the mud into the harder ground beneath. | Gündelik dilde pier "iskele" anlamına da gelir; köprü bağlamında "ara ayak". |
| abutment | kenar ayağı | "There's significant section loss on the lower chord at the third panel point, and the bearings at the south abutment haven't moved freely since about 1975." | Teknik ve gündelik dil karşıtlığı: Lena hemen aynı şeyi gündelik dille söyler: "So it's a bit rusty underneath, and it's got stiff knees." |
| towpath | çekme yolu, nehir kıyısı yolu | By midday I had left Brenning behind and was following the towpath south between fields of young wheat. | Eskiden atların kayık çektiği nehir kenarı yolu; bugün yürüyüş ve bisiklet yolu. |
| let alone | şöyle dursun | And then she had spent forty years working in the drawing office of a firm that made agricultural machinery, and as far as anyone knew she had never designed, let alone built, a single bridge. | Olumsuz cümlede daha da olası olmayan şeyi ekler: "yapmak şöyle dursun, tasarlamamıştı bile". |
| mind you | şunu da söyleyeyim, ama | "Stone's the same, mind you." | Konuşma dilinde bir önceki söze küçük bir düzeltme ya da ek getirir. |
| at arm's length | kol mesafesinde, uzatarak | He studied it for a long time, holding it at arm's length, and then he handed it back with a look of quiet respect. | Mecazen "mesafeli durmak" anlamı da vardır (keep someone at arm's length). |
| go the long way round | uzun yoldan dolaşmak | The people on the far bank have to go the long way round, nearly two hours, for the doctor or the market or the school." | İngiliz kullanımı round; Amerikan İngilizcesinde around. |
| between ourselves | aramızda kalsın | "We call it the Bridge Fair," said Margit, "but between ourselves, the old people still call it Hedda's Day." | Sır ya da gayriresmî bilgi verirken. |
| grid reference | harita koordinatı (pafta referansı) | "A grid reference," I said slowly. | Haritadaki ızgara çizgilerine göre bir noktanın yerini veren rakam grubu. |
| Rarely had I seen | pek nadiren görmüştüm | Rarely had I seen anyone draw a bridge so honestly, and I spend most of my working life looking at drawings of bridges. | Devrik yapı: olumsuz/kısıtlayıcı zarf (rarely, never, seldom) başa gelince yardımcı fiil özneden önce gelir. **Dikkat:** ~~Rarely I had seen~~ yanlış. |
| Not only had I never asked | yalnızca sormamakla kalmamıştım | Not only had I never asked Hedda that question, but I had never even thought of it as a question. | Devrik yapı: Not only + yardımcı fiil + özne, ... but (also) .... Başka örnek: Not only had Hedda drawn a bridge across the river at Wrenmouth; she had designed one. |
| Never had I felt | hiç hissetmemiştim | Never had I felt quite so foolish about a project. | Devrik yapı; I had never felt so foolish cümlesinin vurgulu, edebî biçimi. |
| Hardly had I finished | daha yeni bitirmiştim ki | Hardly had I finished the drawing when he brought out a pot of tea and a map, and asked me, in the tone of a man who has very little to do, where I was heading next. | Hardly / Scarcely + had + özne + V3 ... when. **Dikkat:** hardly ile when kullanılır, ~~than~~ değil (than, No sooner ile gelir). |
| Only then did I understand | ancak o zaman anladım | Only then did I understand what I had been carrying in my bag for two weeks. | Only then / Only when başa gelince devrik yapı: did + özne + yalın fiil. ~~Only then I understood~~ yanlış. |
| What I needed was | bana gereken şey ... idi | What I needed was to get on a bicycle and look. | Wh- cleft: What + özne + fiil + be + vurgulanan bilgi. Başka örnekler: What surprised me was not the bridge but my drawing of it. What had changed was the world around them. |
| it was a dog that found it | onu bulan bir köpek oldu | In the end, it was a dog that found it. | It-cleft: It + be + vurgulanan öğe + that/who. Türkçede vurgu "... olan X oldu" ile korunur. |
| It was Ms Dunmore who | ... Bayan Dunmore oldu | It was Ms Dunmore who got me into the town council. | Vurgulanan öğe insan olduğunda who da kullanılır. Başka örnek: It was Margit who mentioned Tomasz. |
| If I had stopped in Lindhaven, I would not now be sitting | Lindhaven'da dursaydım, şimdi oturuyor olmazdım | If I had stopped in Lindhaven, I would not now be sitting in a strange village with my leg on a cushion. | Karma koşul: geçmişteki koşul (If + past perfect) → şimdiki sonuç (would + yalın fiil / would be + -ing). **Dikkat:** ~~If I would have stopped~~ yanlış; if'li yan cümlede would kullanılmaz. |
| I would now be | şimdi ... olurdum | If she had simply explained the eleventh bridge in her flat that evening, I would now be at home with a cup of tea and two working knees. | Karma koşulun sonuç kısmı; now sözcüğü sonucun şimdiye ait olduğunu gösterir. |
| Having finished my drawing | çizimimi bitirdikten sonra | Having finished my drawing, I showed him Hedda's. | Ortaç yan cümlesi: Having + V3 = önce biten eylem. Ana cümlenin öznesi (I) ortacın da öznesidir. |
| Left alone | baş başa kalınca, yalnız kalınca | Left alone with the wind and the reeds, I found myself having long, rather unfair conversations with my great-aunt in my head. | Edilgen anlamlı ortaç (V3) cümle başında: When I was left alone ... cümlesinin kısa biçimi. |
| Coming down a short slope | kısa bir yokuştan inerken | Coming down a short slope into the village, I braked on a patch of wet cobbles. | -ing ortacı: aynı anda olan eylem. **Dikkat:** ortacın öznesi ana cümleninkiyle aynı olmalı (~~Coming down the slope, the wheel slid~~ yanlış anlam verir). |
| was supposed to take | kullanmam gerekiyordu | "I've got leave I was supposed to take last year. | be supposed to: beklenen, kurala göre yapılması gereken (ama çoğu kez yapılmayan) şey. Başka örnek: "Is it supposed to be boring?" |
| needn't have come | gelmene gerek yoktu (ama geldin) | "You needn't have come all this way for me," she said. | needn't have + V3: eylem yapıldı ama gereksizdi. didn't need to: gerek yoktu (yapılıp yapılmadığı belirsiz ya da yapılmadı). |
| might well have been | pekâlâ ... olabilirdi | Strings of paper lanterns had been looped along both rails, and a brass band was practising something that might well have been a waltz, though it was difficult to be certain. | might well have + V3: geçmişe dair güçlü olasılık. Başka örnek: "I might well have," she said. |
| should have stopped | durmalıydım | I should have stopped in Lindhaven. | Pişmanlık / eleştiri: should have + V3. Başka örnek: "You should have told me." |
| could have guessed | tahmin edebilirdim | A man at the next table thought the old bridge had been "knocked down for the lorries", which I could have guessed. | could have + V3: geçmişte mümkün olan (ama gerek kalmayan) şey. |
| I would rather you heard it from her | onu ondan duymanı tercih ederim | But I would rather you heard it from her. | would rather + başka özne + past simple: şimdiye dair tercih (gerçek dışı geçmiş biçimi). Geçmiş için: I would rather he had told me. |
| I would rather you didn't feel sorry for me | bana acımamanı tercih ederim | "And I would rather you didn't feel sorry for me. | Gerçek dışı geçmiş: biçim geçmiş (didn't), anlam şimdi. **Dikkat:** ~~I would rather you don't~~ yanlış. |
| It was high time I went home | eve dönmemin tam zamanıydı | It was high time I went home, I told myself. | It's (high) time + özne + past simple: "çoktan ... -meliydi". **Dikkat:** ~~It's high time I go~~ yanlış. |
| as if it were floating | sanki süzülüyormuş gibi | The bridge simply stood on a ruled line, as if it were floating in the air. | as if + past simple / were: gerçek dışı karşılaştırma. Resmî dilde I/he/she/it ile de were kullanılır. |
| It is said that | ... söylenir | It is said that my great-aunt once silenced an entire planning committee simply by looking at it, and I could see how that might have happened. | Kişisiz edilgen: It is said / believed / thought that + cümle. Başka örnek: It is said that you can see the weather coming in the delta a full day before it arrives. |
| is believed to have been swept away | sürüklenip gittiğine inanılıyor | It is believed to have been swept away in the great flood of 1968, along with a barn, several sheep and, according to local legend, a piano. | Kişisiz edilgen, özne + be believed + to have + V3 (geçmişe dair). Edilgen mastar: to have been swept. |
| So did I | ben de (öyle yaptım) | "So did I, once," she said. | Eksiltme: So + yardımcı fiil + özne = "ben de". Olumsuz için: Neither had the harbour master (o da duymamıştı). |
| I hope not | umarım (öyle) olmaz | "I hope not," I said. | Yerine koyma: not tüm olumsuz cümlenin yerini tutar. **Dikkat:** ~~I don't hope so~~ yanlış. |
| I'd rather not | tercih etmem, etmesem iyi olur | "I'd rather not," I said, not altogether truthfully. | Eksiltme: I'd rather not (cry). Kibar bir "hayır". |
| had been looking | bakıyordum (bir süredir) | I had been looking at them for two weeks without seeing them. | Past perfect continuous: geçmişteki bir ana kadar süregelen eylem. Başka örnekler: I had been riding for nine days. I had been sending her photographs every evening. |
