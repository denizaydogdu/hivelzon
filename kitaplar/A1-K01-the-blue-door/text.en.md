# The Blue Door
> A1-K01 · Gizem · H-069 pilot metni (taslak, kullanıcı onayı bekliyor).
> Kurallar: cümle ≤ 8 kelime, present simple, yan cümle yok. İşaretler: docs/KITAPLIK.md §6.2.

## [c1] The New Home

### [p01]
Lina is eleven. Today she has a new home.

It is an old building on a river. It has five floors. Lina and her mum live on floor four.

Lina runs up the steps. Every door has a number.

On the top floor, there is one door. It is blue. It has no number.

Lina tries the door. It does not open. It is locked.

Who lives behind the blue door?

### [p02]
Mum carries boxes into the flat. Lina helps her.

In one box, there is a red pot. There is a flower in it. The flower is very dry. It is brown, not green.

"Poor flower," says Lina.

She puts it on her window. Mum looks at it.

"It needs sun," says Mum. "And water."

Lina gives it water. But her window is small. There is not much sun here.

### [p03]
In the evening, Lina meets two neighbours.

Mr Tom lives on floor one. He has a big grey cat. Mrs Ada lives on floor two. She is old and small. She has a kind face.

"Who lives behind the blue door?" asks Lina.

Mr Tom laughs. "No one lives there. It is very old. No one has a key."

Mrs Ada smiles. "No one lives there, dear. That is true."

Then she goes into her flat.

### [p04]
At night, Lina can't sleep. Her new room is strange.

It is nine o'clock. The building is quiet and dark. Lina opens her door. She looks up the steps.

There is a light under the blue door!

It is a warm yellow light. Lina does not move. She waits and watches. At ten o'clock, the light goes off.

In the morning, Lina runs up again. There is something brown on the steps. It is in front of the blue door.

Lina touches it. It is earth.

## [c2] Clues

### [p05]
Lina has a notebook. She writes in it.

One: a light at nine o'clock.

Two: earth on the steps.

She looks at the steps again. There is more earth. It goes down to floor three. Then it goes down to floor two. It stops at a green door.

It is the door of Mrs Ada.

### [p06]
Mrs Ada opens the door. Her flat smells of tomatoes.

"Good morning, Lina," she says.

Lina looks at her hands. They are green!

"Your flower is very dry," says Mrs Ada. "The flower in the red pot."

Lina is surprised. Her flower is on floor four. How does Mrs Ada know?

### [p07]
"Mrs Ada, who lives behind the blue door?" asks Lina.

Mrs Ada smiles again. "No one, dear. And I go to bed early. I sleep at eight."

She closes her door. Lina sits on the steps. She opens her notebook.

Three: green hands.

Four: a smell of tomatoes.

Five: "I sleep at eight."

Lina thinks. Then she writes a question. Is it true?

### [p08]
That night, Lina does not sleep. She sits behind her door and listens.

At eight o'clock, the building is quiet. At half past eight, it is quiet, too.

At nine, Lina hears someone on the steps. Someone slow. They go up, up, up.

Then Lina hears a key.

The blue door opens.

## [c3] The Light

### [p09]
Lina goes up. She is very quiet. Her heart beats fast.

The blue door is open now. Behind it, there are more steps. They are old and dark.

At the top, there is a yellow light. Lina feels the cold night on her face.

She takes one more step. Then she stops.

She is on the roof!

### [p10]
There are little lights everywhere. Under the lights, there is a garden. There are red tomatoes and long green beans. There are many plants and flowers.

A small old woman stands in the garden. She gives water to the tomatoes.

It is Mrs Ada!

"Good evening, Lina," she says. She is not surprised.

"You sleep at eight?" asks Lina.

Mrs Ada laughs. "Not really. Sorry, dear."

### [p11]
"No one lives here," says Mrs Ada. "Only my tomatoes and beans."

Lina laughs, too. Mrs Ada is right!

Then Mrs Ada opens her hand. There are two keys in it.

"One key is for me," she says. "One key is for you."

"For me? Why?"

Mrs Ada looks down. Lina looks down, too. She sees her small window. She sees her red pot.

"I see your poor flower every day. It needs sun. The sun is here."

Now Lina's flower is on the roof. It is next to the tomatoes.

A week later, there is a new leaf.

Lina smiles. She has a key. She has a garden. And she has a friend.
