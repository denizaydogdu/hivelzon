# Sözlük: A Strongly Worded Letter (C2-K03)

İleri kelimeler, deyimler, resmî mektup kalıpları ve C1/C2 dilbilgisi yapıları; Türk öğrenci notları. Taslak; kullanıcı onayı bekliyor (H-028).

| Kelime | Türkçe | Örnek | Not |
|---|---|---|---|
| reluctance | gönülsüzlük, isteksizlik | Reluctance, in her experience, was a word that did a great deal of quiet work. | Sıfatı reluctant (gönülsüz). with the greatest possible reluctance = mümkün olan en büyük gönülsüzlükle: resmî mektupta "bunu yazmak istemezdim ama…" demenin kibar yolu. |
| yew | porsuk ağacı | The hedge stood between her garden and the garden of Hollybank, the house next door, and it was a yew of great age and greater ambition. | Çok yavaş büyüyen, iğne yapraklı, yüzyıllarca yaşayabilen ağaç; İngiliz bahçelerinde sık çit olarak kullanılır. |
| struck the right note | doğru tonu tutturdu | It was the eleventh, with its two commas standing guard on either side of the reluctance, that finally struck the right note. | strike → struck. Deyim: bir konuşma ya da yazıda tam uygun havayı yakalamak. |
| with respect | tüm saygımla (ama…) | The phrase "with respect" was, she knew, one of the most dangerous in the English language. | Pasif-agresif kibarlık: arkasından hemen hep bir itiraz gelir. With the greatest respect daha da serttir. |
| I trust you will | ...-eceğinize güveniyorum | "I trust you will forgive the length of this letter. | Resmî mektup kalıbı; kitapta açıklandığı gibi çoğu zaman "yapacaksınız" anlamına gelir. |
| Yours sincerely | Saygılarımla | Yours sincerely, Desmond Achterberg." | Alıcının adıyla başlayan (Dear Mr Achterberg) İngiliz resmî mektubunun kapanışı. Adı bilinmiyorsa (Dear Sir or Madam) Yours faithfully kullanılır. |
| taking liberties | haddini aşmak, laubalilik etmek | One did not prune other people's property; it was the sort of thing her mother would have called "taking liberties". | take the liberty of doing something = izin almadan bir şey yapmak: I have taken the liberty of trimming… |
| put it down to | -e yordu, -e bağladı | If it had occurred to her to wonder why Mr Pike cut her side as carefully as his own, she had put it down to his good manners, and to the fact that a hedge cut on one side only looks like a man who has shaved half his face. | put something down to something = bir şeyin nedenini bir şeye bağlamak. |
| shears | budama makası | "But I shouldn't throw away your shears, Miss Webb." | Her zaman çoğul: a pair of shears = bir budama makası (scissors gibi). ~~a shear~~ denmez. |
| surveyor | ölçüm uzmanı, harita mühendisi | The surveyors will come on Thursday morning. | Arazi, sınır ve bina ölçen uzman; survey = ölçüm, inceleme (bölüm adı The Survey). |
| to the point of cruelty | zalimlik derecesinde | When they are truly angry, they become courteous to the point of cruelty. | to the point of + isim = ... derecesine varacak kadar. İngiliz kibarlığı üzerine kuru bir gözlem. |
| trespassed | izinsiz sınırı geçmişti | The letter said only: "Dear Miss Webb, I return herewith a rose of yours which, I regret to inform you, had trespassed. | trespass = başkasının arazisine izinsiz girmek; burada bir gül için kullanılması ironiktir. herewith = ekte (çok resmî). |
| marmalade | marmelat, portakal reçeli | Constance had thanked him each year with a jar of her marmalade, and he had thanked her for the marmalade, and that had been the full extent of their relationship. | İngilizcede marmalade yalnız turunçgil (portakal, limon) reçelidir; çilek reçeli jam'dir. ~~strawberry marmalade~~ denmez. |
| Never had she needed | hiç ihtiyaç duymamıştı | Never had she needed eleven drafts. | Devrik yapı (C1): olumsuz zarf (Never, Not once, Never in her life) cümle başına gelince yardımcı fiil öznenin önüne geçer: had she. ~~Never she had needed~~ yanlıştır. |
| Only then did she | ancak o zaman | Only then did she feel calm enough to continue. | Devrik yapı: Only then / Only when ... + did + özne + fiil. Türkçede vurgu "ancak o zaman" ile korunur. |
| Not until | ancak ... olunca | Not until the cup in front of her had gone completely cold did Constance move. | Devrik yapı: Not until + yan cümle + did + özne + fiil = "ancak ... olunca ... -di". |
| It was the phrase | ... olan bu ifadeydi | It was the phrase "in good order" that kept her awake that night. | Cleft (vurgulu) yapı: It was X that ... = ... olan X'ti. Vurgulanan öğe öne alınır. |
| What she needed | ona gereken | What she needed, she decided over the marmalade, was not an opinion but a fact. | Cleft yapı: What + özne + fiil + was ... = "... -en şey ... idi". |
| would not now be lying | şimdi yatıyor olmazdı | If she had not written that first letter, he would not now be lying in a hospital in Kingsmere with pins in his wrist. | Karma koşul (C1): geçmişteki koşul (If + past perfect) + şimdiki sonuç (would + now + V). Türkçede "-seydi, şimdi ... olmazdı". |
| Having considered them | onları düşündükten sonra | Having considered them, she decided that they proved nothing whatever, except that whoever had put them there in 1952 had been as careless with his measurements as Mr Achterberg was with his titles. | Ortaç yan cümlesi: Having + V3 = "-dikten sonra". Öznesi ana cümlenin öznesiyle aynı olmalıdır (she). |
| ought not to have needed | gerekmemesi gerekirdi | Its shadow reached her roses at four and her conservatory at five, by which time Constance was usually sitting there with the crossword and a cardigan she ought not to have needed. | ought (not) to have + V3 = geçmişte olması (olmaması) gereken şey. Güneş olsa hırkaya gerek kalmayacaktı. |
| needn't have bought | almasına gerek yokmuş | She needn't have bought the tin of cat food; the cupboard was full of them, in neat rows, labelled by date. | needn't have + V3 = yapıldı ama gereksizdi. ~~didn't need to have bought~~ değil; didn't need to buy ise "almadı, gerek yoktu" anlamına gelir. |
| might well have | pekâlâ ... -ebilirdi | It might well have been because nobody believed she would answer truthfully. | might well have + V3 = geçmişe dair güçlü olasılık. |
| I would rather you didn't | ... -memeni tercih ederim | "I would rather you didn't tell me what to do with my marmalade." | Gerçek dışı geçmiş: would rather + özne + geçmiş zaman = başkasının yapmasını (yapmamasını) istemek. Anlam şimdiki zamandır. |
| high time | çoktan vakti gelmiş | "All I'm saying," said Beatrice gently, "is that it's high time one of you knocked on the other's door. | It's (high) time + özne + geçmiş zaman: anlam şimdiki zamandır, "artık ... -menin vakti geldi". ~~it's high time one of you knocks~~ yanlış. |
| It was said | söylendiğine göre | It was said in the village shop that Mr Achterberg had been an inspector of taxes. | Kişisiz edilgen: It is / was said that ... = "... söyleniyor / söylenirdi". |
| He was believed to have spent | geçirdiği sanılıyordu | He was believed to have spent thirty-eight years reading other people's accounts and finding them wanting. | Kişisiz edilgen: özne + be believed to have + V3 = "... -dığı sanılıyor". |
| So, she suspected, was Priya | Priya da öyleydi | So, she suspected, was Priya, whose takings had never been better. | Eksiltme ve yerine koyma: So + yardımcı fiil + özne = "... da öyle". Önceki cümledeki was enjoying himself tekrarlanmaz. |
| Little did she know | hiç haberi yoktu | Little did she know, as she heard it drop into the dark of the box, that she had just begun the longest correspondence of her life. | Öne alma (C2): Little + did + özne + know = "hiç bilmiyordu". Anlatıcı okura geleceği sezdirir. |
| Such was the heat | öyle kızışmıştı ki | Such was the heat of the exchange that Barry began to carry a bottle of water. | Öne alma: Such was + isim + that ... = "öyle ... idi ki". |
| So completely had she been wrong | öyle tümüyle yanılmıştı ki | So completely had she been wrong that there was almost something restful about it. | Öne alma ve devrik yapı: So + zarf + yardımcı fiil + özne ... that. |
| Failure to maintain | bakımının yapılmaması | Failure to maintain a boundary hedge in good order is, after all, the kind of thing that leads to disputes between neighbours. | Adlaştırma (C2): fiil yerine isim (failure, reduction, disturbance, examination). Vergi müfettişinin ve ölçüm raporunun kurumsal dili. |
| It would appear that | görünüşe göre | "It would appear that the copy supplied with the sale particulars of Hollybank was produced in reverse, a fault not uncommon in copies of this age, and that the T therefore appears on that copy to stand on the Hollybank side. | Temkin dili (hedging): kesin konuşmaktan kaçınma. Benzerleri: arguably, I would venture to suggest. |
| Should you wish | isterseniz | "Should you wish to discuss the matter further, I would be glad to receive your views in writing. | If'siz şart (C2): Should + özne + fiil = If you wish. Resmî mektup dili. |
| Had I known | bilseydim | "Had I known the true position, I would never have written to you at all. | If'siz şart: Had + özne + V3 = If I had known. |
| Were it not for | ... olmasaydı | Were it not for my own stubbornness, you would not have climbed that ladder, and you would not now be in Kingsmere with pins in your wrist. | If'siz şart: Were it not for + isim = If it were not for. İkinci yarısı karma koşuldur (would not now be). |
| be settled | çözülmesi | Before any decision is made, it is essential that the question of ownership be settled. | Resmî dilek kipi (subjunctive): It is essential / I propose that + özne + yalın fiil (be, be carried out). ~~is settled~~ değil. |
| That said | bununla birlikte | That said, the blackbirds will have left by August, whereas the hedge, I fear, will not. | Söylem belirleyicisi: önceki söze bir karşı nokta ekler. Benzerleri: Even so, Granted, By the same token. |
| By the same token | aynı şekilde | By the same token, I would never presume to cut a hedge that was not my own. | Söylem belirleyicisi: "aynı mantıkla". Sonun ironisi: Desmond tam olarak bunu yapıyordu. |
| He's being | (şu sıralar) ... davranıyor | "He's being a bit difficult about the food, to be honest. | Görünüş inceliği: be + being + sıfat = geçici davranış. He is difficult (huyu zor) ile He's being difficult (şu an zorluk çıkarıyor) farklıdır. |
| I was wondering whether | acaba ... diye merak ediyordum | "Dear Mr Achterberg, I was wondering whether my last letter had perhaps failed to reach you, since I have not had the pleasure of a reply." | Görünüş inceliği: geçmiş sürekli zaman ricayı ve soruyu yumuşatır; doğrudan soru sormaktan daha kibardır. |
| I've been meaning to | bir süredir ... -meyi düşünüyorum | I've been meaning to ask him what the disagreement was about, but he always changes the subject." | Present perfect continuous + mean to = bir süredir niyet edip yapamamak. |
| agree | katılmak, aynı fikirde olmak | You will, I am sure, agree. | **Dikkat:** agree bir fiildir: I agree, you will agree. ~~I am agree~~, ~~you will be agree~~ yanlıştır. |
| information | bilgi | On Saturday Beatrice went to the village shop for milk and came back an hour later with a loaf of bread, a great deal of information and an expression of enormous satisfaction. | **Dikkat:** information sayılamaz: a great deal of information, a piece of information. ~~informations~~, ~~an information~~ yanlıştır. |
| Mrs | (evli kadına) Bayan | "Seventy-four years of being Miss Webb, and he makes me Mrs in a single line." | Miss = evlenmemiş kadın, Mrs = evli kadın, Ms = evlilik durumu belirtmeyen hitap; hepsi soyadıyla kullanılır (Miss Webb). İngiliz yazımında noktasız: Mr, Mrs, Ms. |
