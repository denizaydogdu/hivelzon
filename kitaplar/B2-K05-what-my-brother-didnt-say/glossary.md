# Sözlük: What My Brother Didn't Say (B2-K05)

Seviye üstü kelimeler (CEFR-J B2 listesi dışında) ve Türk öğrenci notları (B2 dilbilgisi yapıları). Taslak; kullanıcı onayı bekliyor (H-028).

| Kelime | Türkçe | Örnek | Not |
|---|---|---|---|
| boatyard | tekne atölyesi, tersane | At the end of it, there is nothing except rocks, long grass and the old boatyard, which closed years ago. | Listede yok. boat + yard (avlu): teknelerin yapıldığı ya da onarıldığı yer. |
| apprentice | çırak | My first apprentice in thirty years. | Listede yok. Bir ustanın yanında iş öğrenen kişi. Çıraklık: apprenticeship. |
| sawdust | talaş | The floor was covered in sawdust and curly pieces of wood. | Listede yok. saw (testere) + dust (toz). |
| fishermen | balıkçılar | There were fishermen, neighbours, two of my teachers and a lot of children who had been told that there might be cake. | Listede yok. Tekili fisherman; çoğulu düzensiz: fishermen (man → men). |
| gulls | martılar | The sea was grey and flat, and the gulls were already complaining about something. | Listede yok. Tekili gull; seagull da denir. |
| scraping | kazıma (sesi) | From inside the shed came a sound I didn't recognise at first: a long, smooth, scraping sound, again and again, like someone breathing slowly. | Listede yok. scrape = kazımak, sıyırmak. |
| hoover | elektrik süpürgesi | She turned on the hoover before I could ask anything else. | Listede yok. İngiliz günlük dili; genel kelime vacuum cleaner. |
| plaster | yara bandı | There was a plaster on his thumb. | Listede yok. İngiliz İngilizcesi; Amerikan İngilizcesinde band-aid. "Alçı" anlamı da vardır. |
| flask | termos | Mrs Kelly from the post office was standing at the front with a flask of tea, so the whole town must have heard about it by Friday night. | Listede yok. a flask of tea = bir termos çay. |
| became | oldu, -leşti | Mr Doyle suddenly became very interested in a tool on the wall. | Düzensiz fiil: become → became → become. become interested in = -e ilgi duymaya başlamak. |
| halfway | yarı yolda | Halfway there, she turned the radio off. | half + way. halfway there = oraya giderken yolun yarısında. |
| lit | ışığı yanan | He looked up at the lit window of Mum and Dad's room. | light → lit → lit. Sıfat olarak: a lit window = ışığı yanan pencere. |
| shone | parladı | The new wood shone like honey, and the old wood at the bottom looked dark and deep, like the root of a strong tree. | Düzensiz fiil: shine → shone → shone. |
| feet | ayaklar | Dad walked down to the ramp slowly, as if the ground might move under his feet. | foot'un düzensiz çoğulu: foot → feet (~~foots~~ değil). |
| had been falling | yağıyordu (o zamandan beri) | It was a Tuesday evening in late April, and the rain had been falling since lunchtime. | Past perfect continuous: geçmişteki bir ana kadar süregelen eylem. Türkçede "-iyordu" ile söylenir. |
| have been worrying | endişeleniyoruz (bir süredir) | Your mother and I have been worrying about you all week. | Present perfect continuous: have / has been + -ing. Başlayıp hâlâ süren eylem. **Dikkat:** ~~We are worrying about you all week~~ yanlıştır; süre varsa (all week, for, since) present perfect continuous kullanılır. |
| must have left | çıkmış olmalı | He must have left very early. | Geçmişe dair güçlü tahmin: must have + V3. Türkçe "-miş olmalı". |
| can't have been going | gidiyor olamaz | He can't have been going to a lecture, I thought. | Geçmişe dair "imkânsız" çıkarımı: can't have + V3 (ya da been + -ing). ~~mustn't have~~ bu anlamda kullanılmaz. |
| might have found | bulmuş olabilir | He might have found work at the fish market. | Geçmişe dair zayıf olasılık: might / may / could have + V3. |
| if I had been | olsaydım | If I had been a more patient person, I would have waited for him to tell me. | 3. tip koşul: If + past perfect, would have + V3. Gerçekleşmemiş geçmiş. Türkçe "-saydı, -ırdı". |
| none of this would have happened | bunların hiçbiri olmazdı | If Daniel had told them the truth in April, none of this would have happened. | 3. tip koşulun sonuç kısmı: would have + V3. ~~would happened~~ değil. |
| if you told him | ona söylesen | If you told him the truth, he would at least know you were working. | 2. tip koşul: If + past simple, would + yalın fiil. Şimdi ya da gelecek için gerçek dışı / olasılığı düşük durum. |
| should have said | demeliydim | I should have said no. | Pişmanlık: should have + V3 = "-meliydim (ama yapmadım)". |
| I wish | keşke | Although I wish he'd hurry up. | wish + would: başkasının davranışından duyulan sıkıntı (keşke acele etse). wish + past simple / could: şimdiye dair dilek (I wished I could tell him). |
| if only | keşke | If only he'd come just once. | if only, wish'ten daha güçlü bir dilek ya da özlemdir. Burada he'd = he would. |
| told me he was fine | iyi olduğunu söyledi | He told me he was fine, but people who are fine don't come home in the middle of the term with two bags. | Dolaylı anlatım: "I'm fine" → he told me he was fine (zaman bir adım geriye kayar). tell'den sonra kişi gelir (told me); say'den sonra gelmez (~~said me~~). |
| asked him whether | olup olmadığını sordu | I asked him whether he had a girlfriend, whether he owed anyone money, whether he had joined a band. | Dolaylı soru: evet/hayır sorusu whether ya da if ile aktarılır; soru sırası düz cümleye döner (~~whether did he have~~ değil). |
| asked me to turn | kapatmamı istedi | He said no to everything and then asked me to turn the light off on my way out. | Dolaylı rica / emir: ask / tell + kişi + to + yalın fiil (told me to stop). |
| has had the ramp cleared | rampayı temizletti | Mr Doyle has had the ramp cleared, and the life jackets have been checked twice. | Ettirgen yapı: have + nesne + V3 = işi başkasına yaptırmak. Türkçedeki "-tir / -dir" ekinin karşılığı. |
| have been checked | kontrol edildi | Mr Doyle has had the ramp cleared, and the life jackets have been checked twice. | Present perfect edilgen: have / has been + V3. Diğer edilgen örnekleri: it was left here, the photo had been taken, will be put in the water. |
| will have learned | öğrenmiş olacak | By next summer, Dad will have learned to use his old tools again, and Daniel will be building his second boat. | Future perfect: gelecekteki bir ana kadar tamamlanmış olacak eylem (by + zaman). |
| will be building | yapıyor olacak | By next summer, Dad will have learned to use his old tools again, and Daniel will be building his second boat. | Future continuous: gelecekte bir anda sürüyor olacak eylem. Başka örnek: We'll be putting her in the water at ten. |
| whereas | oysa, -ken | Mum talks when she is nervous, whereas Dad goes silent. | İki durumu karşılaştırır. although / though da zıtlık bildirir: although it was still raining. |
| despite | -e rağmen | Despite everything, he looked happier than I had seen him in years. | despite + isim ya da -ing (despite the rain, despite being tired); arkasından tam cümle gelmez. Cümle için although kullanılır. ~~despite of~~ yanlış. |
| however | ancak, ise | The bottom part, however, was darker, almost brown, as if it had been waiting there much longer than the rest. | however iki cümleyi bağlamaz; virgülle ayrılır ve cümlenin başında, ortasında ya da sonunda durabilir. |
