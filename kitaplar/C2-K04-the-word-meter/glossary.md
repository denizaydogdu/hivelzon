# Sözlük: The Word Meter (C2-K04)

İleri kelimeler, kitapta türetilmiş sözcükler, deyimler ve C1/C2 dilbilgisi yapıları (her yapı için metinden en az bir örnek), Türk öğrenci notlarıyla. Taslak; kullanıcı onayı bekliyor (H-028).

| Kelime | Türkçe | Örnek | Not |
|---|---|---|---|
| word-broker | kelime komisyoncusu | She was a word-broker, and a good one. | Kitapta türetilmiş: word + broker (komisyoncu, simsar). Gerçek İngilizcede stockbroker (borsa simsarı), insurance broker gibi kullanılır. |
| talk-debt | konuşma borcu | After that, every word you spoke went down as talk-debt, to be repaid out of the following days, with a fine on top. | Kitapta türetilmiş: talk + debt (borç). go down as = (kayda) … olarak geçmek. |
| allowance | hak, ödenek, kota | She signed that she wished to see the reports on the spoken allowance. | Resmî dil. Günlük dilde pocket money yerine (İngiliz) allowance da denir. |
| dormant | uykuda, durgun, hareketsiz | A dormant account. | Bankacılıkta "hareketsiz hesap". Yanardağ için de kullanılır: a dormant volcano. |
| enforcement | denetim, yaptırım, uygulama | It was he who had recommended that the enforcement of the allowance be retired. | enforce (bir kuralı zorla uygulamak) fiilinin adı. law enforcement = kolluk. |
| beside the point | konu dışı, önemsiz | The arithmetic of the thing was beside the point. | Deyim. ~~besides the point~~ değil: besides = ayrıca. |
| a man of few words | az konuşan bir adam | Even before the silence, he had been a man of few words. | Kalıp. Kitabın ironisi: kelimelerin sayıldığı bir şehirde bile "az konuşan" diye anılır. |
| took it personally | üstüne alındı | Joaquim laughed, a big, warm laugh that filled the little room and went out into the Arcade, where a pigeon took it personally and left. | take something personally = bir şeyi üstüne alınmak, kişisel algılamak. Burada bir güvercin için şaka yollu. |
| in so many words | açık açık, tam bu sözlerle | It was the first time she had admitted it to herself in so many words, and it went through her like cold water. | Çoğunlukla olumsuz cümlede: He didn't say it in so many words = Açıkça söylemedi. |
| apropos of nothing | durup dururken, konuyla hiç ilgisi yokken | Today, in a warm kitchen in Eastmere, with the kettle on for the fourth time and her father saying, apropos of nothing, that he had been meaning to paint the front door for eleven years, she was busy. | Fransızcadan gelen resmî bir kalıp; okunuşu "apropo". |
| Never had she been | Hiç … olmamıştı (devrik yapı) | Never had she been so busy and achieved so little. | C1 devrik yapı: olumsuz zarf (Never, Rarely) başa gelince yardımcı fiil özneden önce gelir. ~~Never she had been~~ değil. |
| Only then did she understand | Ancak o zaman anladı (devrik yapı) | Only then did she understand what her father had said to her on her eighteenth birthday. | Only then / Only when + yardımcı fiil + özne. Geçmiş zamanda did eklenir, fiil yalın kalır: ~~Only then she understood~~ değil. |
| Hardly had she seen it when | Bunu görür görmez (devrik yapı) | Hardly had she seen it when something in her chest went loose, like a knot coming undone. | Hardly / Scarcely … when; No sooner … than. ~~Hardly had she seen it than~~ değil: hardly ile when kullanılır. |
| What he needed, it turned out, was | Anlaşıldı ki ona gereken şey … idi (cleft) | What he needed, it turned out, was a loan of four thousand words, repayable over a year at the usual rate. | C1 vurgulu yapı (what-cleft): vurgulanan bilgi sona, "was"tan sonraya gelir. |
| It was he who | … olan oydu (cleft) | It was he who had written the final report, at that same kitchen table, over a whole winter, because he was not a man who found writing easy. | It-cleft. Resmî dilde It was he who; günlük dilde It was him who da duyulur. |
| If she had gone five years ago | Beş yıl önce gitmiş olsaydı (karma koşul) | If she had gone five years ago, she would not now be sitting on the edge of her bed in the dark, holding a notebook she could no longer see, counting the words of a speech she knew by heart. | Karma koşul: geçmişteki koşul (had gone) + şimdiki sonuç (would now be). Türkçede "-seydi, şimdi … -ıyor olmazdı". |
| Having asked it | Onu sorduktan sonra (ortaç yan cümlesi) | Having asked it, he had not waited for an answer. | Having + V3 = önce olan eylem. Öznesi ana cümleninkiyle aynı olmalı (he). |
| Left alone with | … ile baş başa kalınca (ortaç yan cümlesi) | Left alone with the back of his cardigan, Petra looked around her. | Edilgen ortaç (V3) cümle başında: "Yalnız bırakılmış olarak". Sırtını dönen babaya ironik bir gönderme. |
| needn't have worried | endişelenmene gerek yoktu (ama endişelendin) | You needn't have worried. | needn't have + V3 = yapıldı ama gereksizdi. Dikkat: ~~didn't need to worry~~ farklıdır; o, "gerek yoktu (ve yapmadın)" demektir. |
| would rather have swallowed | yutmayı tercih ederdi | Petra would rather have swallowed the slip. | would rather + have + V3 = geçmişte başka bir şeyi tercih ederdi. Kitapta da sık: She would rather not have taken the paper. |
| I'm supposed to be | olmam gerekiyor (bekleniyor) | Tomorrow I'm supposed to be in Eastmere. | be supposed to = plana, kurala ya da beklentiye göre gerekmek. Dikkat: ~~I'm suppose to~~ değil; -d düşmez. |
| It is said that | … söylenir (kişisiz edilgen) | It is said that Petra's grandmother heard it once, in a queue at the post office, but then it is said that Petra's grandmother saw a great many things. | Kişisiz edilgen: söyleyen belli değil. Metinde benzerleri: It was said in Eastmere that…, Numbers, it was felt, would… |
| it was high time Petra had | Petra'nın … olmasının vakti çoktan gelmişti (gerçek dışı geçmiş) | Mrs Varga signed that it was high time Petra had an occasion, and went on down the stairs with an expression of deep satisfaction, as if she had arranged the whole thing herself. | It's (high) time + geçmiş zaman: anlam şimdiki zamandır ("artık … olmalı"). as if + past perfect de gerçek dışıdır. |
| I would rather you hadn't | Keşke yapmasaydın (gerçek dışı geçmiş) | I would rather you hadn't. | would rather + başka özne + past perfect = geçmişte başkasının yapmamış olmasını isterdim. Eksiltili: hadn't (stood across the street). |
| so could she | o da yapabilirdi (eksiltme) | If he could be silent, so could she. | so + yardımcı fiil + özne = "o da". Olumsuzda neither: neither could she. |
| I hope not | Umarım değildir (yerine koyma) | "I hope not," said Joaquim cheerfully. | not tüm bir cümlenin yerini tutar. ~~I don't hope so~~ denmez; olumlu hâli I hope so. |
| Such was the shock that | Şaşkınlık öyle büyüktü ki (öne alma) | Such was the shock that she spoke aloud, there in the hall, without meaning to. | C2 öne alma: Such + be + özne + that. Metinde ayrıca: Such is the comfort of a rule that… |
| Little had she suspected | Hiç şüphelenmemişti (öne alma) | Little had she suspected, sitting under his workbench handing him screws, what it was that he mended. | Little olumsuz anlamlı zarftır ve devrik yapı ister: ~~Little she had suspected~~ değil. |
| So freely did the words come | Kelimeler öyle serbestçe geliyordu ki (öne alma) | So freely did the words come that neither of them noticed when the clock passed three, and then four, and then five, and the window over the sink began to turn from black to grey. | So + sıfat/zarf + yardımcı fiil + özne + that. Edebî ve vurgulu. |
| the cancellation of | -in iptali (adlaştırma) | In the interests of administrative simplicity and the reduction of record-keeping costs, the Service has approved the cancellation of all accumulated allowances. | C2 adlaştırma: fiil (cancel) yerine ad (cancellation). Resmî duyuru dili; eylemi yapan gizlenir. |
| Failure to report one | Birini bildirmemek (adlaştırma) | Failure to report one was grounds for the immediate withdrawal of a broker's licence. | failure to + fiil = "-memek" (resmî). Duyuruda da: failure to make use of… |
| It would appear | Görünen o ki (temkin) | It would appear, it said, that the objectives of the spoken allowance have been substantially achieved. | C2 temkinli dil (hedging): yazar sorumluluğu üstlenmeden yargı bildirir. Bölüm sonunda anlatıcı aynı kalıbı ironik kullanır. |
| One might suggest | … ileri sürülebilir (temkin) | One might suggest that the habits of quiet are now so firmly established among the population that the measure no longer depends on enforcement for its effect. | Genel özne one + might: kesinlikten kaçınma. Benzerleri: Arguably, …; It could be argued that… |
| It is essential that every adult citizen be fitted | Her yetişkin yurttaşa … takılması şarttır (resmî dilek kipi) | It is essential that every adult citizen be fitted with a meter, and that the measure be reviewed annually. | It is essential / vital that + özne + yalın fiil (be, -s yok). ~~is fitted~~ değil. Metinde: recommended that the enforcement … be retired. |
| Had she come at noon | Öğlen gelmiş olsaydı (if'siz şart) | Had she come at noon, she would have had time for every word. | If she had come = Had she come. Resmî ve edebî; if düşer, had başa gelir. |
| Should the citizens continue | Yurttaşlar … sürdürecek olurlarsa (if'siz şart) | Should the citizens continue to observe the allowance, they will be observing something that is no longer there. | Should + özne + yalın fiil = If the citizens continue (olasılık düşük, resmî). |
| Were it not for Joaquim | Joaquim olmasaydı (if'siz şart) | Were it not for Joaquim, I would not even have known to look. | If it were not for = Were it not for. Geçmiş için: Had it not been for… |
| That said | Öte yandan, yine de (söylem belirleyicisi) | That said, it is also true that she had now rehearsed the opening of the speech some two hundred times, and that going to a kiosk was something to do which was not, strictly speaking, going to Eastmere. | Önceki söze bir çekince ekler. Benzerleri: Even so, Having said that. |
| Granted | Kabul, doğru (söylem belirleyicisi) | Granted, Petra thought, that was not a crime. | Karşı görüşün bir kısmını kabul eder, ardından genellikle "ama" gelir. |
| by the same token | aynı şekilde, aynı nedenle | And by the same token, you would never be able to tell yourself that you could not afford to come. | Önceki gerekçenin başka bir sonucunu bağlar. token = jeton, belirti. |
| he's being difficult | (şu an) huysuzluk ediyor (görünüş inceliği) | Don't mind him, he's being difficult. | be being + sıfat = geçici davranış. He is difficult = huysuz biridir (kalıcı). |
| I was wondering whether | merak ediyordum (görünüş inceliği) | I was wondering whether you would knock, or simply stand across the street until morning. | Past continuous, bir düşüncenin süregittiğini ve yumuşak bir tonu gösterir. Rica için de: I was wondering if you could… |
| had been meaning to | (uzun süredir) -meyi düşünüyordu | Today, in a warm kitchen in Eastmere, with the kettle on for the fourth time and her father saying, apropos of nothing, that he had been meaning to paint the front door for eleven years, she was busy. | I've been meaning to … = bir süredir yapmayı düşünüyorum (ama yapmadım). Kitabın kendi teması gibi: niyet edilmiş ama ertelenmiş. |
| had been saving hers for eleven years | on bir yıldır kendininkileri biriktiriyordu | In the city of Halvern, every citizen was allowed nine hundred spoken words a day, and Petra had been saving hers for eleven years. | **Dikkat:** süre için for, başlangıç noktası için since: ~~since eleven years~~ değil. "-dır" Türkçesi İngilizcede present/past perfect continuous ister. |
| listened to it | onu dinledi | He listened to it without blinking. | **Dikkat:** listen to + nesne: ~~listened it~~ değil. Türkçedeki "-i dinlemek" İngilizcede edat ister. |
