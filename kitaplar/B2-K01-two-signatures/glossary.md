# Sözlük: Two Signatures (B2-K01)

Seviye üstü kelimeler (CEFR-J B2 listesi dışında) ve Türk öğrenci notları. Taslak; kullanıcı onayı bekliyor (H-028).

| Kelime | Türkçe | Örnek | Not |
|---|---|---|---|
| varnish | vernik | Under the varnish of an ordinary harbour painting, Lucía found a second name. | Listede yok. Tabloyu korumak için sürülen saydam kat; yıllar içinde sararır ve koyulaşır. |
| varnished | verniklenmiş; vernikledi | Someone added it around 1930, and then it was varnished over. | varnish fiil olarak da kullanılır. varnish over something = bir şeyin üstünü verniklemek. |
| restoration | restorasyon, onarım | It happened on a grey Tuesday in October, in a small restoration workshop on a narrow street behind the harbour of Marell. | Listede yok. restore = eski hâline getirmek; restorer = restoratör. |
| intern | stajyer | She was twenty-six, and she had been working as an intern for Mr Castell since the summer. | Listede yok. Staj: internship. |
| attic | çatı katı, tavan arası | It had been found in the attic of an old house on the hill, which was being emptied after its owner had died. | Listede yok. |
| archive | arşiv | In the morning, before work, she went to the city archive. | Listede yok. Vurgu ilk hecede: AR-kive. |
| archivist | arşivci | The archivist, Ms Roig, was a thin woman with silver hair who spoke in a whisper even when nobody else was in the room. | Listede yok. archive + -ist (artist, chemist gibi). |
| shelves | raflar | Ms Roig disappeared between the shelves and came back twenty minutes later with three boxes and an expression of mild surprise. | Tekili shelf. -f / -fe ile biten bazı isimlerde çoğul -ves olur: shelf → shelves, wife → wives. |
| frowned | kaşlarını çattı | She frowned. | Listede yok (frown). Kızgınlık, şaşkınlık ya da düşünme ifadesi. |
| ultraviolet | morötesi (UV) | In the cupboard under the sink there was an ultraviolet lamp, which Mr Castell used to check for old repairs. | Listede yok. Gözle görülmeyen ışık; restoratörler eski onarımları ve alt katmanları görmek için kullanır. |
| easel | şövale | In front of her stood an easel. | Listede yok. Ressamın tuvali koyduğu ayaklı sehpa. |
| bookseller | kitapçı (second-hand bookseller: sahaf) | A tired man told her that the furniture had been sold, the rubbish had been thrown away and the books and papers had been bought by a second-hand bookseller in the old town, a Mr Pons. | book + seller. |
| bundle | deste, tomar | At the bottom of the third box there was a bundle of letters tied with a faded blue ribbon. | Listede yok. a bundle of letters = bir deste mektup. |
| loop | halka, ilmek | It's the same A, with the same little loop. | Listede yok. |
| nineteenth | on dokuzuncu | Painters in the nineteenth century were not always honest. | **Dikkat:** 19. yüzyıl = 1800'ler (1801–1900). Türkçedeki gibi sıra sayısıyla söylenir: the nineteenth century. |
| doctorate | doktora | My friend Tomás is doing a doctorate in chemistry at the university. | Listede yok. do a doctorate in = ... alanında doktora yapmak (~~make a doctorate~~ değil). |
| titanium | titanyum | It contains a white made from titanium. | Listede yok. Titanyum beyazı, ressamlara 1920'lerden sonra yaygın olarak satılan bir beyaz boyadır. |
| had been working | çalışıyor(du) (-dır / -dı) | She was twenty-six, and she had been working as an intern for Mr Castell since the summer. | Present / past perfect continuous: geçmişte başlayıp süren eylem. Türkçede şimdiki zaman + -dır ile söylenir: "yazdan beri çalışıyor". ~~She is working since the summer~~ yanlıştır. |
| had been found | bulunmuştu | It had been found in the attic of an old house on the hill, which was being emptied after its owner had died. | Edilgen (passive): be + V3. Eylemi yapan önemli değilse kullanılır. Past perfect edilgen: had been + V3; sürerken: was being + V3. |
| has never been seen | hiç görülmedi | He has never been seen at an exhibition in his life, and I am afraid he never will be. | Present perfect edilgen: has / have been + V3. |
| he said that | ... olduğunu söyledi | He said that he had always suspected it, but that it made no difference. | Dolaylı anlatım: zaman bir geri kayar (has suspected → had suspected, makes → made). say + (that), tell + kişi: He told me that... (~~He said me~~ değil). |
| asked whether | -ip -mediğini sordu | She phoned me last month and asked whether I ever found anything by Serra. | Dolaylı soru: if / whether + özne + fiil (soru sırası yok: ~~asked did I find~~ değil). |
| told her to finish | bitirmesini söyledi | He told her to finish the sky by Thursday and to leave the signatures alone. | Dolaylı emir: tell + kişi + to + fiil. Olumsuz: tell someone not to... |
| if he had lived there | orada yaşasaydı | If he had lived there, he would have been counted. | 3. tip koşul: if + had + V3, would have + V3. Geçmişte olmamış bir şeyi hayal eder. |
| would have disappeared | kaybolmuş olurdu | And if the painting had been sold on Saturday, the woman on the wall would have disappeared into a private house, perhaps forever. | 3. tip koşulun sonuç kısmı. Türkçede çoğu zaman "-ecekti" ya da "-miş olurdu" diye çevrilir. |
| if Ms Vidal had wanted | Bayan Vidal isteseydi | If Ms Vidal had wanted to cheat them, why would she pay so much? | Karışık koşul: geçmişteki koşul (had wanted) + şimdiki sonuç (would she pay). |
| if people knew the truth | insanlar gerçeği bilse | He told me that if people knew the truth, nobody would want them. | 2. tip koşul: if + geçmiş zaman, would + fiil. Şimdi gerçek olmayan durum. ~~if people would know~~ değil. |
| wished she had | keşke ... olsaydı diye diledi | She hadn't thought of that, and she wished she had. | wish + past perfect: geçmişe dair pişmanlık. Kısaltılmış biçim: she wished she had (thought of it). |
| if only | keşke | "If only my grandmother could see this," she whispered. | if only + could / geçmiş zaman: şimdiye dair güçlü dilek. wish'ten daha duygusaldır. |
| must have been standing | duruyor olmalıydı | The painter must have been standing on the other side of the harbour, Lucía realised, somewhere near the beginning of Rope Street. | Geçmişe dair çıkarım: must have + V3 = "kesin öyle olmuş", might have + V3 = "olmuş olabilir". |
| can't have seen | görmüş olamaz | She can't have seen the boat in them. | can't have + V3 = "kesinlikle olmamış". Geçmiş için ~~mustn't have~~ kullanılmaz. |
| have the old books scanned | eski defterleri taratmak | We have the old books scanned by an outside company. | Ettirgen: have + nesne + V3 = işi başkasına yaptırmak. Türkçedeki -tır / -dır ekinin karşılığı: taratmak, onartmak. |
| had the frame repaired | çerçeveyi onarttı | The museum had the frame repaired and the woman on the harbour wall photographed under ultraviolet light, so that visitors could see her too. | Ettirgen geçmiş zaman: had + nesne + V3. |
| she'll be taking it home | onu eve götürüyor olacak | If she likes it, she'll be taking it home at the weekend, and we'll be paying the rent on time for once. | Future continuous: will be + -ing; gelecekte bir anda sürmekte olan eylem. |
| will have written | yazmış olacak | By then, your friend will have written his report, and you will have cleaned that second name properly. | Future perfect: will have + V3; by + zaman ile "o zamana kadar bitmiş olacak". |
| despite | -e rağmen | Her hands felt cold, despite the heat in the little shop. | despite + isim (despite the heat). ~~despite of~~ yanlıştır; although + cümle: although they were not dirty. |
| however | ne var ki, oysa | However, the whole thing was covered in a thick yellow-brown varnish, so dark that the sea looked like soup and the sky looked like cold tea. | Cümle başında virgülle; iki cümle arasındaki zıtlığı bağlar. |
| which | ki o (virgüllü ilgi cümlesi) | In the cupboard under the sink there was an ultraviolet lamp, which Mr Castell used to check for old repairs. | Tanımlamayan ilgi cümlesi virgülle ayrılır ve ek bilgi verir; burada that kullanılmaz. |
