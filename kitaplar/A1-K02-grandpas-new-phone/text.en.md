# Grandpa's New Phone
> A1-K02 · Mizah · H-071 metni (taslak, kullanıcı onayı bekliyor).
> Kurallar: cümle ≤ 8 kelime, present simple, yan cümle yok; emir kipi, can / can't, haftanın günleri. İşaretler: docs/KITAPLIK.md §6.2.

## [c1] Monday to Wednesday

### [p01]
Grandpa Kemal is seventy-two. He lives in Ankara.

He has a small flat. He has a big laugh. And he has a new phone. It is black and thin.

This week, Grandpa has one job. On Saturday, it is Ece's birthday. Ece is his granddaughter.

Grandpa wants to send her one nice photo. It is easy, right?

But the new phone does everything wrong.

Today is Monday. Saturday is five days away.

### [p02]
On Monday morning, Grandpa sits on his sofa. He looks at the phone. The phone looks at him.

"Call Ece," he says to the phone.

The phone rings. Then a man answers.

"Good morning! This is the bread shop!"

"The bread shop?" says Grandpa. "No, no. Sorry!"

The man laughs. "Kemal, is that you? Your big cake is ready!"

"What cake?" asks Grandpa. But the call ends.

"Oh no," he says.

### [p03]
On Tuesday, Grandpa opens his window. The city is beautiful today.

He can see Mert's window. Mert is fourteen. He lives on the same floor. His window has a blue curtain.

Today Mert's flat is full of noise. Many people laugh in there.

Grandpa wants a photo of the city. He touches the phone. But the photo is not the city. It is his foot!

"Oh no!" says Grandpa.

Then the phone makes a happy noise. Where is the photo now? Grandpa does not know.

### [p04]
On Wednesday, Grandpa goes to the park. He sits with his friends. They drink tea and play a game.

Then music comes from his pocket. Dance music! Birds fly up. Everyone looks at Grandpa.

He touches everything. The music does not stop. Now he has photos of his ear. And the sky.

His friends laugh. "Kemal, is that your new phone?"

"Yes," says Grandpa. "My new phone hates me."

In the evening, the phone rings. It is Ece! He touches the red button.

The call ends.

"Oh no! Ece!"

## [c2] Thursday and Friday

### [p05]
On Thursday morning, the bell rings. It is Mert.

"Good morning, Grandpa Kemal! Can I help with your phone?"

Grandpa is surprised. "My phone? How do you know?"

Mert's face is red. "I hear the music. In the park."

Grandpa thinks. Mert is not in the park on Wednesday.

"Come in," says Grandpa. "Do you want tea?"

### [p06]
They sit at the kitchen table. Mert takes the phone.

"Look, Grandpa. This is the camera. Touch here. Smile. Then touch Send."

Grandpa tries. It is a good photo of Mert.

"I can do it!" says Grandpa.

"Now, Ece calls you," says Mert. "What do you do?"

"I open the phone?" asks Grandpa.

Mert laughs. "No, Grandpa. You answer the phone. Touch the green button, not the red one."

### [p07]
On Friday, Grandpa tries again alone. Touch here. Smile. But then he touches something new.

There are many faces on the phone. Ece, her mum, her dad and Uncle Murat!

"Grandpa!" they say. They look very surprised.

Grandpa looks at the faces. Every face has a blue curtain behind it.

"Why is everything blue?" asks Grandpa.

Ece laughs. "Blue is a nice colour, Grandpa! Bye!"

The faces go away.

### [p08]
In the evening, Grandpa sits at his window. Mert's flat is quiet now.

Grandpa thinks about the week. A big cake. A lot of noise. Mert and the music. And blue, blue, blue.

He goes to bed early. Tomorrow is the big day. He can do it!

On Saturday morning, Grandpa wears his best shirt. He has yellow flowers for the photo.

Then the bell rings.

## [c3] Saturday

### [p09]
"One minute!" says Grandpa.

First, the photo. He holds the flowers and the phone.

Touch here. Smile.

It is Grandpa with the flowers. It is a nice photo! He touches Send.

Behind the door, there are many happy noises. Phones!

Grandpa stops. What is that?

### [p10]
Grandpa opens the door.

Ece is there with her mum and dad. Uncle Murat and the cousins are there. Mert is there, too. The man from the bread shop is there. He has a very big cake.

"Surprise!" they say.

"But it is your birthday, Ece," says Grandpa.

Ece kisses him. "Yes, Grandpa. And I want my birthday here. With you."

"We are in Mert's flat all week!"

Grandpa looks at Mert. "The blue curtain!"

Mert smiles. "Yes. My curtain. And your phone is my job."

### [p11]
The family carries the party into the flat.

On the wall, there are many photos. His ear. The sky. And his foot!

"Your phone sends us every photo," says Ece.

"Oh no," says Grandpa.

But the family loves the photos. Uncle Murat likes the sky. Mert likes the ear. Everyone loves the foot!

Grandpa shows them his nice photo.

"It is nice, Grandpa," says Ece. "But I love the foot."

Grandpa laughs. His new phone does everything wrong. But today, wrong is right.
