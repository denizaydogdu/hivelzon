# Sözlük: The Lake That Remembered Tomorrow (C1-K04)

İleri düzey kelimeler, deyim ve eşdizimler, C1 dilbilgisi yapıları ve Türk öğrenci notları. Taslak; kullanıcı onayı bekliyor (H-028).

| Kelime | Türkçe | Örnek | Not |
|---|---|---|---|
| hydrologist | hidrolog, su bilimci | Dr Hallam, his director, had made it clear that the post of senior hydrologist was his if the report was clean, quick and convincing. | hydro (su) + -logist (bilimci). senior = kıdemli; make it clear that = açıkça belli etmek. |
| a fortnight | iki hafta | He had arrived the evening before, on the last bus of the day, with two cases of instruments, one small suitcase and a firm intention to leave within a fortnight. | İngiliz İngilizcesi; "fourteen nights"tan gelir. Amerikan İngilizcesinde two weeks denir. |
| guesthouse | pansiyon | "You're the man from the Water Office," said the woman who opened the guesthouse door. | Küçük, aile işletmesi konaklama yeri. |
| anomaly | anomali, olağandışılık | Possible visual anomaly on the water surface, lasting approximately three seconds. | Bilimsel üslup: özne ve fiil düşürülmüş not cümlesi. Çoğulu anomalies. |
| fatigue | yorgunluk | The most likely explanation is fatigue combined with suggestion. | Resmî ve bilimsel; gündelik dilde tiredness. Okunuşu /fəˈtiːɡ/. |
| weigh it up | tartmak, artısını eksisini düşünmek | Tomás did not think about it, did not weigh it up, did not decide. | Ayrılabilen öbek fiil: weigh up the options / weigh it up. Zamirle mutlaka araya girer (~~weigh up it~~ değil). |
| primed | önceden koşullandırılmış | It was the kind of thing anyone might well have done on such a morning, and he had been primed, as psychologists say, by the picture he had imagined the day before. | Psikoloji terimi: bir uyarıcıyla zihnin önceden hazırlanması. |
| outflow | çıkış ağzı, gölün boşaldığı yer | He knew how much water the northern streams could carry, and how quickly it could leave through the narrow outflow under the old stone bridge. | out + flow (akış). Karşıtı inflow. |
| ledger | kayıt defteri | Then he described the ledger: its age, its method, the eighty years of entries kept by three generations of the same family, the number of reflections, the sixty-one per cent. | Asıl anlamı muhasebe defteri; burada düzenli tutulan kayıtlar. |
| blank | boş | Oriana's grandmother had marked these with a single word in the margin: blank. | blank page = boş sayfa; my mind went blank = aklım durdu. Kitabın son bölümünün adı: Blank Water. |
| made up his mind | kararını verdi | Constantin, who had sat up all night and made up his mind, had seen only sky. | make up one's mind = karar vermek. **Dikkat:** ~~made his mind~~ değil; up zorunlu. sit up all night = bütün gece uyumadan oturmak. |
| drifted into | -e sürüklendi | He had drifted into it, one small easy step after another, the way he had drifted into giving away his coat. | drift = akıntıyla sürüklenmek; mecazen karar vermeden bir duruma kayıp gitmek. Kitabın anahtar kelimesi. |
| per cent | yüzde | Sixty-one per cent. | İngiliz yazımı iki kelime (per cent); Amerikan İngilizcesi percent. **Dikkat:** Türkçedeki gibi önce yazılmaz: ~~per cent sixty-one~~ değil, sixty-one per cent. |
| It would appear that | … görünmektedir, … anlaşılmaktadır | It would appear that the reported images do not represent future events as such, but rather the observer's strongest current desire. | Temkinli (hedging) bilimsel üslup: kesin iddiadan kaçınır. Benzerleri: it seems that, apparently. |
| arguably | belki de, rahatlıkla denebilir ki | It was arguably the best idea he had had in years. | Temkinli dil: "Tartışılabilir ama savunulabilir." |
| unremarkable | olağan, dikkate değer olmayan | The physical and chemical characteristics of the lake are unremarkable. | un- + remarkable. Raporlarda "normal" demenin resmî yolu. |
| premature | erken, vakitsiz | In the author's view, the dismissal of these reports without further study would be premature. | Adlaştırma (the dismissal of…) ile birlikte resmî üslup. Rapor yazarı kendinden "the author" diye söz eder. |
| Never had he felt | hiç … hissetmemişti | Never had he felt so cold, and the frost was the least of it. | Devrik yapı (G-C1-01): olumsuz zarf başa gelince yardımcı fiil özneden önce gelir. **Dikkat:** ~~Never he had felt~~ yanlıştır. the least of it = işin en önemsiz yanı. |
| Only when the bus had disappeared did Tomás understand | ancak otobüs gözden kaybolunca Tomás anladı | Only when the bus had disappeared did Tomás understand what he had done. | Devrik yapı: Only when … ile başlayan cümlede ana cümle devrilir (did + özne + yalın fiil). ~~Tomás understood~~ değil. |
| Hardly had he reached | varır varmaz | Hardly had he reached the shore the next morning when he heard voices. | Hardly had + özne + V3 … when … = "-ir -mez". **Dikkat:** hardly ile when kullanılır (~~than~~ değil; than, no sooner ile gelir). |
| Not once did they disagree | bir kez bile anlaşmazlığa düşmediler | Not once did they disagree about what actually had to be done. | Devrik yapı: Not once / Not until / Not only başa gelince did + özne + yalın fiil. |
| Only much later did he realise | ancak çok sonra fark etti | Only much later did he realise that it was perhaps the most important observation he made in Ardal, and that he had made it while thinking about something else. | Devrik yapı. realise İngiliz yazımı (Amerikan: realize). |
| Not only were they normal | yalnızca normal değillerdi | Not only were they normal; they were so typical that they could have been used to teach students what an ordinary mountain lake looked like. | Devrik yapı: Not only + be + özne. Cümlenin ikinci yarısı genellikle but (also) ile ya da noktalı virgülle gelir. |
| It was Tomás who | … olan Tomás'tı | It was Tomás who had been chosen to go and prove otherwise. | Cleft yapı (G-C1-02): It was + vurgulanan öğe + who/that … Türkçede "seçilen kişi Tomás'tı" gibi vurgu sona kayar. |
| What disturbed him was not | onu rahatsız eden şey … değildi | What disturbed him was not that he had given away his coat. | Cleft yapı: What + cümle + be + vurgulanan öğe. Benzer: What he needed, he decided, was a theory. |
| If he had been the kind of man | … türden bir adam olsaydı | If he had been the kind of man who threw things away, he would not be a candidate for senior hydrologist now. | Karma koşul (G-C1-03): geçmişe (ya da kalıcı kişiliğe) dair koşul (had been) + şimdiki sonuç (would not be … now). |
| If you hadn't been watching | izliyor olmasaydınız | If you hadn't been watching that machine of yours, my kitchen would be full of lake now. | Karma koşul: dün geceki koşul (past perfect continuous) + şimdiki sonuç (would be … now). that machine of yours = o makineniz (hafif şakacı). |
| Had I told you | size söyleseydim | Had I told you, you would have written it down as a charming local story and gone home a week early. | Devrik koşul: if düşer, had başa gelir (= If I had told you). Resmî ve edebî. |
| Should anyone on the board ask | kuruldan biri olur da sorarsa | Should anyone on the board ask about them, you can say they were outside the scope of the study. | if'siz koşul: Should + özne + yalın fiil = If anyone should ask (olasılığı düşük). |
| Having lived in Ardal all her life | bütün ömrünü Ardal'da geçirmiş biri olarak | Having lived in Ardal all her life, she did not need to be told twice that the lake could be dangerous. | Ortaç yan cümlesi (G-C1-04): Having + V3 = ana eylemden önce tamamlanmış, sebep bildiren durum. Öznesi ana cümleninkiyle aynı olmalı. |
| Reading them back | onları yeniden okurken | Reading them back, he felt slightly sick. | Ortaç yan cümlesi: -ing ile eşzamanlı eylem. read back = yazdığını yeniden okumak. |
| needn't have come | gelmenize gerek yokmuş | You needn't have come all this way. | Modal incelik (G-C1-05): needn't have + V3 = yapıldı ama gereksizdi. **Dikkat:** didn't need to come çoğu zaman "gelmedin, çünkü gerek yoktu" demektir. |
| might well have done | pekâlâ yapmış olabilirdi | It was the kind of thing anyone might well have done on such a morning, and he had been primed, as psychologists say, by the picture he had imagined the day before. | might well = oldukça olası (might'tan güçlü). Benzer: He might well have no position at all by the end of next week. |
| ought to have felt | hissetmesi gerekirdi | He ought to have felt relieved. | ought to have + V3 = beklenen ama gerçekleşmeyen durum (should have ile aynı anlam, biraz daha resmî). |
| supposed to | -mesi gereken, -mesi beklenen | In both pictures his reflection was crouching too, as reflections are supposed to do. | be supposed to = kurala ya da beklentiye göre yapılması gereken. Benzer: That's what I'm supposed to do. |
| I'd rather you didn't | etmemeni tercih ederim | In fact, I'd rather you didn't. | Gerçek dışı geçmiş (G-C1-07): would rather + başka özne + geçmiş zaman, şimdiki bir dileği anlatır. **Dikkat:** ~~I'd rather you don't~~ değil. Aynı özneyle yalın fiil: I'd rather have a quiet one. |
| It's high time | zamanı çoktan geldi | It's high time this whole business was put to rest. | Gerçek dışı geçmiş: It's (high) time + geçmiş zaman, anlamı şimdi. **Dikkat:** ~~It's high time this business is put~~ değil. put to rest = kapatmak, son vermek. |
| as if it were a warning | sanki bir uyarıymış gibi | She said his name as if it were a warning. | as if + were (bütün öznelerle) gerçek dışı karşılaştırma. Gündelik dilde was de duyulur; resmî yazıda were. |
| It is said that | … söylenir | It is said that mountain villages are slow to trust strangers. | Kişisiz edilgen (G-C1-06): It is said / believed / thought that … Genel kanıyı aktarır. |
| is said to have been | -diği söylenen | A young man saw himself telling a girl at the summer dance that he loved her, and did, rather badly, according to Oriana's grandmother, who is said to have been at the dance herself. | Kişisiz edilgenin kişili biçimi: özne + is said + to have + V3 (geçmişe dair). |
| It is recommended that | … önerilir | It is recommended that no decision on the water plan be taken until a full season of observation has been completed. | Resmî dilek kipi: recommend / suggest / insist that + yalın fiil (be taken). **Dikkat:** ~~is taken~~ değil. |
| So, I imagine, do you | sanırım sen de (istiyorsun) | So, I imagine, do you. | Eksiltme ve yerine koyma (G-C1-08): So + yardımcı fiil + özne = "… de öyle". Burada araya I imagine (sanırım) girmiş. |
| Neither would I | ben de inanmazdım | Neither would I, at your age. | Olumsuz cümleye katılma: Neither / Nor + yardımcı fiil + özne. Benzer: Nor had he decided to stay. |
| I hope not | umarım (öyle) değil | I hope not. | Yerine koyma: not tüm olumsuz yan cümlenin yerini tutar. Olumlusu: I expect so (sanırım öyle). **Dikkat:** ~~I don't hope so~~ denmez. |
