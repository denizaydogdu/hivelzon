# Sözlük: The Other Half of the Sentence (C2-K05)

İleri düzey kelimeler ve deyimler, metindeki Türkçe kelimeler, babanın bilinçli olarak kusurlu İngilizcesi (Türk öğrencinin tipik hataları) ve C1/C2 dilbilgisi yapıları. Taslak; kullanıcı onayı bekliyor (H-028).

| Kelime | Türkçe | Örnek | Not |
|---|---|---|---|
| hüzün | hüzün | There is a word in Turkish, hüzün, which dictionaries translate as sadness and which is not sadness at all. | Metinde Türkçe bırakılmıştır. İngilizcede tam karşılığı yoktur; melancholy ve sadness yakın ama eksik kalır. |
| gönül | gönül | Gönül is the heart, but not the one that pumps blood. | Türkçe kelime. İngilizcede heart ile karşılanır, ama "istek, razı olma" anlamı kaybolur. |
| kıyamamak | kıyamamak | Then there was kıyamamak, which is the worst of all, because it has no English cousin at all. | Türkçe kelime. En yakın İngilizce: can't bear to (harm / spend / use up). |
| estranged | arası açık, uzaklaşmış | A novel with nine chapters is a problem, whereas a novel with nine chapters and a final chapter by the author's estranged daughter is a story, and stories sell. | Aile üyeleri için: an estranged daughter / father. Resmî ve ağır bir kelimedir. |
| false friend | yanıltıcı benzer kelime ("sahte arkadaş") | A translator would call that a false friend: two words in two languages that look alike and mean different things. | Türk öğrenci için örnekler: sympathetic ≠ sempatik (anlayışlı demektir), actually ≠ aktüel (aslında demektir). Kitaptaki örnek: vapur / vapour. |
| recuse herself | kendini (davadan) çekmek | Arguably, this is the point at which a translator ought to recuse herself, in the way that a judge does when the defendant turns out to be her cousin. | Hukuk dili: recuse oneself. Burada mizahi üslup kayması. |
| syllabus | müfredat, ders programı | Dilek Hanım put her glass down and folded her hands in her lap, the way teachers do when they are about to say something that is not on the syllabus. | not on the syllabus = programda olmayan; mecazen "kitapta yazmayan". |
| pushchair | bebek arabası | I was standing at the window, watching a man across the road try to fold a pushchair that had opinions of its own. | İngiliz İngilizcesi; Amerikan İngilizcesinde stroller. |
| fortnight | iki hafta | Underneath was a list I had made for him one summer, apparently convinced that I could teach him in a fortnight what my mother had failed to teach him in eleven years. | İngiliz İngilizcesi (fourteen nights). Amerikan İngilizcesinde two weeks. |
| I am agree | (kusurlu) katılıyorum | "I am agree," said Dilek Hanım, with the weary affection of a woman who has heard a sentence several hundred times. | **Dikkat:** ~~I am agree~~ yanlıştır; doğrusu I agree. agree bir fiildir, be ile kullanılmaz. Türkçedeki "aynı fikirdeyim" kalıbından gelir. Kitapta babanın bilinçli seçimidir: "agreement is a place where you are". |
| Everybody already know | herkes zaten biliyor | Everybody already know it is him. | **Dikkat:** ~~Everybody know~~ → Everybody knows. everybody tekildir; geniş zamanda fiile -s gelir. Türk öğrencinin en sık hatası. |
| only few word | yalnızca birkaç kelime | In your language I have only few word. | **Dikkat:** ~~few word~~ → a few words. Çoğul -s ve a eksik. (few = çok az; a few = birkaç.) Babanın onuncu bölümünde bilinçli olarak korunur. |
| I am write | (kusurlu) yazıyorum | "This chapter I am write in your language, because in my language I am always arrive too late." | **Dikkat:** ~~I am write~~ → I am writing ya da I write; ~~I am always arrive~~ → I always arrive. Türk öğrenci am'i Türkçedeki "-(y)im" ekinin karşılığı sanar. |
| Other half is yours | öbür yarısı senin | Other half is yours." | **Dikkat:** the eksik → The other half is yours. Türkçede belirli tanımlık (the) yoktur; bu yüzden ya hiç konmaz ya her yere konur. Bölüm başlığı da tanımlıksızdır: Other Half of Sentence. |
| Little did I know | hiç haberim yoktu | Little did I know, standing on the pavement with my suitcase, how much else had come from that window. | C2 öne alma (fronting) + devrik yapı: Little did + özne + yalın fiil. "Hiç bilmiyordum / haberim yoktu" vurgusu. |
| Such was my certainty that | öyle emindim ki | Such was my certainty that I carried it, mostly unexamined, for twenty-nine years. | C2 öne alma: Such + be + isim + that. Türkçede "öyle … ki" ile verilir. |
| So hard had he pressed | öyle sert bastırmıştı ki | So hard had he pressed on the paper that the crossed-out words could still be read from the other side. | C2 öne alma: So + sıfat/zarf + yardımcı fiil + özne + that. Düz biçimi: He had pressed so hard that … |
| Not once did my father use | babam bir kez bile kullanmamıştı | Not once did my father use my name in those nine chapters. | C1 devrik yapı: olumsuz zarf başa gelince yardımcı fiil özneden önce gelir (did my father use). |
| Only after a week of this did I do | ancak bir hafta sonra yaptım | Only after a week of this did I do the thing I had been avoiding. | C1 devrik yapı: Only after / Only then + yardımcı fiil + özne. Türkçede "ancak … -dikten sonra(dır ki)". |
| Hardly had I put the phone down | telefonu kapatır kapatmaz | Hardly had I put the phone down after one of these calls, at nineteen, when I burst into tears in the corridor of my student hall, and a girl from the floor above asked whether somebody had died. | C1 devrik yapı: Hardly had + özne + V3 … when … = "… -ir -mez". **Dikkat:** ~~Hardly had I … than~~ değil; Hardly … when, No sooner … than. |
| Not only did he refuse | yalnızca reddetmekle kalmadı | Not only did he refuse to give up, it seemed, but he also refused to give up his mistakes. | C1 devrik yapı: Not only + yardımcı fiil + özne …, but also … |
| nor would it have satisfied | … da memnun edemezdi | It would not have passed an examination, nor would it have satisfied Dilek Hanım, who had spent three years trying to put the articles back where they belonged. | nor'dan sonra devrik yapı: nor + yardımcı fiil + özne. |
| It was the photocopies that changed everything | her şeyi değiştiren fotokopilerdi | It was the photocopies that changed everything. | C1 cleft (vurgu) yapısı: It was + vurgulanan öğe + that … Türkçede vurgulanan öğe yükleme gider: "… fotokopilerdi". |
| What I had never translated | hiç çevirmediğim şey | What I had never translated, and had privately decided I never would, was a novel by my father. | C1 wh-cleft: What + cümle + be + vurgulanan öğe. Başka örnek: What it cannot do is argue … |
| If I had kept up | sürdürmüş olsaydım | If I had kept up my Turkish as a teenager, I would now be able to read my father's novels without a dictionary. | C1 karma koşul: If + past perfect (geçmiş), would + yalın fiil + now (şimdiki sonuç). Başka örnek: If he had agreed …, I would not be holding these pages now. |
| Having started | başladıktan sonra | Having started, I found that I couldn't stop, which is not something I have ever been able to say about a dishwasher manual. | C1 ortaç yan cümlesi: Having + V3 = "-dıktan sonra". Başka örnek: Having spent most of my adult life … |
| might well have | pekâlâ … olabilir | "He might well have. | C1 modal inceliği: might well have + V3 = "pekâlâ … -miş olabilir" (olasılık yüksek). Burada fiil eksiltilmiş: He might well have (given it to someone). |
| needn't have worried | endişelenmeme gerek yokmuş | I needn't have worried that she would be annoyed. | C1: needn't have + V3 = yapıldı ama gerek yoktu. **Dikkat:** didn't need to = gerek yoktu (ve büyük olasılıkla yapılmadı). |
| supposed to be | -mesi gerekir, -mesi beklenir | I want to describe exactly what I felt at that moment, because it seems important, and because I am supposed to be good with words. | C1: be supposed to = beklenti, görev. Geçmişte gerçekleşmeyen plan: He was supposed to come to you the following April. |
| is believed to have written | yazdığı sanılıyor | My father is believed to have written the whole book in the last two years of his life, at the kitchen table, with the window open, although nobody, not even his publisher, had been allowed to read a page of it until after the funeral. | C1 kişisiz edilgen: özne + is believed / said + to have + V3 (geçmişe dair). |
| It is said that | … söylenir | It is said that every translation is a small betrayal. | C1 kişisiz edilgen: It is said that … Başka örnek: People are said to be too old to change. |
| It's high time | vakti çoktan geldi | "It's high time somebody in this family finished something." | C1 gerçek dışı geçmiş: It's (high) time + özne + past simple (şimdi için). **Dikkat:** ~~It's high time somebody finishes~~ değil. |
| as if the glass were very large | sanki bardak çok büyükmüş gibi | He was pouring tea, and he went on pouring it, slowly, as if the glass were very large. | C1 gerçek dışı: as if / as though + past (were). Resmî İngilizcede bütün öznelerle were. |
| would rather I didn't tell you | söylememi istemezdi | "Your father," he said at last, "would rather I didn't tell you this." | C1: would rather + özne + past simple = başkasının davranışına dair tercih. Yanıt eksiltili: I would rather you did. |
| I'd rather not | yapmasam daha iyi | "I'd rather not." | C1 eksiltme: I'd rather not (finish it). Başka örnekler: I hope not. So had I. So would I. |
| So had I | ben de öyle | So had I. | C1 yerine koyma: So + yardımcı fiil + özne = "ben de". Yardımcı fiil önceki cümleyle uyumlu olur (had read → So had I). |
| Should you open it | açacak olursan | "Should you open it before the ninth chapter is finished," she said, "I shall not know, of course. | C2 if'siz şart: Should + özne + yalın fiil = If you (should) open it. Resmî. Başka örnek: Should you require any assistance … |
| Had I known | bilseydim | Had I known on that Sunday in Manchester what was waiting in Istanbul, I would have flown out the next morning. | C2 if'siz şart: Had + özne + V3 = If I had known (3. tip koşul, devrik). |
| Were it not for | … olmasaydı | Were it not for Dilek Hanım and her terrible honesty, I would have opened this envelope in a hotel room in Istanbul, alone, with eight chapters behind me instead of nine, and I would not have understood half of what I was about to read. | C2 if'siz şart: Were it not for + isim = If it were not for / If it had not been for. |
| It is essential that the final text be delivered | son metnin teslim edilmesi şarttır | "It is essential that the final text be delivered by the end of March," the email went on, "should the spring publication date be maintained. | C2 resmî dilek kipi: It is essential / I insist + that + özne + yalın fiil (be). Başka örnek: I must insist that the chapter be printed exactly as he wrote it. |
| the postponement of publication | yayımın ertelenmesi | Although I should say," and here her register changed, as smoothly as a car changing gear, "that failure to deliver a complete manuscript by the agreed date would, regrettably, result in the postponement of publication until next autumn at the earliest." | C2 adlaştırma (resmî, kurumsal dil): postpone → the postponement of; fail to deliver → failure to deliver; complete → the completion of; correct → the correction of. Aylin buna "threatening me in nouns" der. |
| It would appear | görünüşe göre | It would appear, however, that we had all been wrong, and that he had meant something much simpler. | C2 temkinli dil: It would appear / It would seem that … Kesin konuşmaktan kaçınır. |
| Arguably | tartışmaya açık olsa da, belki de | Arguably, this is the point at which a translator ought to recuse herself, in the way that a judge does when the defendant turns out to be her cousin. | C2 tutum zarfı. Aynı paragrafta temkin: One might suggest that … |
| By the same token | aynı mantıkla, aynı şekilde | By the same token, every correct word that he had crossed out was a word he knew. | C2 bağdaşıklık belirleyicisi. Kitaptaki diğerleri: Granted, … Even so, … That said, … |
| I'm being difficult | zorluk çıkarıyorum | "I'm being difficult, aren't I? | C2 görünüş inceliği: be being + sıfat = geçici davranış (şu an böyle davranıyorum). Başka örnekler: She was being kind. I'm being seventy-one. He's being impossible. |
| I've been meaning to | bir süredir … yapmayı düşünüyordum | "I've been meaning to ring you," I said, which was true in the sense that I had picked up the phone four times and put it down again. | C2 görünüş inceliği: have been meaning to = niyetim vardı ama yapmadım (kibar özür). Aynı sahnede: I was wondering if … (kibar soru). |
