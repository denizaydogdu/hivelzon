# Pip Cannot Sleep
> A1-K04 · Bilimkurgu/fantastik · H-071 metni (taslak, kullanıcı onayı bekliyor).
> Kurallar: cümle ≤ 8 kelime, present simple, yan cümle yok. İşaretler: docs/KITAPLIK.md §6.2.
> Anlatıcı "I" kitabın sonunda Pip çıkar; ilk cümle bu yüzden "I tell you a story tonight."

## [c1] Night

### [p01]
I tell you a story tonight. It is about a robot. I know this robot very well.

He is small. He has two arms and one wheel. His eyes are little blue lights. His name is Pip.

Pip lives in a yellow house. He lives with a family. There is a mum and a dad. There is a girl, too. Her name is Zara. She is eight.

Pip can do many things. But he can't do one thing.

What can't Pip do?

### [p02]
Every evening, Pip works in the house. He washes the cups. He cleans the kitchen floor. He puts the toys in the box.

At eight o'clock, Zara goes to bed. Mum reads her a story. Dad says, "Good night, Zara."

At ten, Mum and Dad go to bed. Pip turns off the lights. One, two, three, four.

Now the house is dark and quiet. Everyone sleeps.

Everyone, but not Pip.

### [p03]
Pip stands in the kitchen. There is a big moon in the window.

"Robots don't sleep," says Pip. "I know that."

But Pip wants to sleep. He wants to close his eyes. He wants to dream.

Zara dreams every night. In the morning, she tells Pip her dreams. She dreams about cats, the sea and cake.

"What is a dream like?" asks Pip.

Pip has no dreams. He only has long, quiet nights.

### [p04]
Pip has a plan. Zara has a book about sleep. The book says, "Count sheep."

So Pip counts sheep. One sheep, two sheep, three sheep.

But Pip is very fast. Ten, twenty, thirty, forty, fifty! Fifty sheep in one second!

Pip is not tired. He is the same.

"Sheep are no good," says Pip.

Then he tries warm milk. Dad drinks warm milk at night. Pip puts milk in a cup. But Pip can't drink. The milk goes on the floor.

Now Pip cleans the floor again.

### [p05]
Then Pip finds a blanket. It is green. It is Zara's old blanket.

Pip puts it on his head. It is too small. He puts it on his wheel. It is too small again.

Pip sits under the window. He is sad. His blue lights are sad, too.

"I can't sleep," says Pip.

It is two o'clock. Then a door opens. It is Zara. She has her toy bear in her arms.

"Pip," says Zara. "I can't sleep."

Pip looks at her. His lights go on and off.

"Me too," says Pip.

## [c2] A Story

### [p06]
Zara sits on the floor with Pip.

"Why can't you sleep?" asks Pip.

"My head is busy," says Zara. "I think and think."

"I try sheep," says Pip. "Fifty sheep in one second. It is no good."

Zara laughs. "Fifty? Pip, that is very fast!"

Then she looks at Pip. "Do you want to sleep, too?"

"Yes," says Pip. "Very much. Every night."

### [p07]
Zara thinks. Then she smiles.

"Mum reads me a story. Then I sleep."

"Can I read you a story?" asks Pip.

"Yes, please!"

They go to Zara's room. Zara gets into bed. Pip puts the green blanket on her. It is the right size for Zara.

On the table, there are many books. Zara gives Pip a small blue book. It is new.

"This one, please," she says. "It is about a robot."

### [p08]
Pip opens the book. He reads the first line.

"I tell you a story tonight."

Pip reads. He is slow and quiet.

The story is about a little robot. He lives in a yellow house. He tries sheep. He tries milk. His blanket is too small.

Zara laughs. "He is like you, Pip!"

Pip reads on. Page one, page two, page three.

### [p09]
At page ten, Zara's eyes close. She sleeps. She smiles in her sleep.

Pip stops. The room is warm and dark. The house is quiet.

Pip feels heavy. His lights go down. Blue, then grey, then dark.

Then something new happens.

Pip sees a green hill. There are white sheep on the hill. One, two, three. They are very slow.

One sheep looks at Pip. "Hello, Pip," it says.

Pip is not in Zara's room. He is in a dream!

### [p10]
In the morning, Zara opens her eyes. Pip is on the floor by her bed. His blue lights are on again.

"Pip! Do you sleep now?"

"Yes," says Pip. "And I dream. I dream about sheep."

Zara laughs. "Fifty sheep?"

"No. Only three. They are my friends."

Zara looks at the blue book. "Pip, look at the name!"

Its name is Pip Cannot Sleep.

Pip looks at the book. Then he looks at you. Yes, you.

Do you know me now? I am Pip. This is my story. I tell it to you tonight.

Are you in bed? Is your blanket warm? Are your eyes tired?

Me too.

Close your eyes. Count the sheep. One, two, three.

Good night.
