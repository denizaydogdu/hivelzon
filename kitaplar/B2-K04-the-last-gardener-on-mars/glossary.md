# Sözlük: The Last Gardener on Mars (B2-K04)

Seviye üstü kelimeler (CEFR-J B2 listesi dışında) ve Türk öğrenci için B2 dilbilgisi notları. Taslak; kullanıcı onayı bekliyor (H-028).

| Kelime | Türkçe | Örnek | Not |
|---|---|---|---|
| colony | koloni (yerleşim) | Life in Kestrel Colony was quiet, organised and careful, because on Mars careless people did not usually get a second chance. | Listede yok. Başka bir gezegende ya da uzak bir yerde kurulan yerleşim. İyelik: the colony's (koloninin). |
| councillor | meclis üyesi, konsey üyesi | "We have a duty to everyone in this colony," said Councillor Hale, a tall, careful man who never raised his voice. | Listede yok. council (konsey) + -lor. Unvan olarak büyük harfle: Councillor Hale. Çoğulu councillors. |
| chamber | oda, hazne | The Test Chamber | Listede yok. Özel amaçlı kapalı oda: test chamber = deney odası. |
| hundredth | yüzüncü (two-hundredth: iki yüzüncü) | On the colony's two-hundredth day of spring, every plant in the greenhouse began to die at once. | Sıra sayısı: hundred → hundredth (yüzüncü). İngilizcede tarih ve gün sıra sayısıyla söylenir: the tenth day, the first autumn. |
| sensors | sensörler, algılayıcılar | The roof sensors had been recording everything, although nobody had ever looked at that particular number, because the computer was only interested in the total amount of light. | Listede yok (sensor). Işık, sıcaklık gibi şeyleri ölçen cihaz. |
| bud | tomurcuk | "It's a bud." | Çiçek ya da yaprağın açılmadan önceki hâli. a flower bud = çiçek tomurcuğu. |
| had them tested | onları test ettirdi | A team in white suits took samples from every row and had them tested in the colony's small laboratory. | **Ettirgen have:** have + nesne + V3 = bir işi başkasına yaptırmak. Türkçedeki -tir/-dir eki: had a set of lamps fitted (lamba taktırdı). ~~had tested them~~ "kendisi test etmişti" demektir. |
| have been planned | planlanıyor (-dır), planlanmış durumda | The power we save will be used for the new homes that have been planned for years. | **Present perfect edilgen:** have/has been + V3. Yıllardır süren ve hâlâ geçerli bir durum. Türkçede şimdiki zamanla söylenir: "yıllardır planlanıyor". |
| was still being brought | hâlâ getiriliyordu | They belonged to Dr Lindqvist, and they had been there since the first years of the colony, when paper was still being brought from Earth. | **Past continuous edilgen:** was/were being + V3 = o sırada yapılmakta olan iş. |
| asked him whether | ona -ip -mediğini sordu | She listened carefully and asked him whether he was absolutely certain. | **Dolaylı soru:** ask + whether/if + düz cümle sırası (~~whether was he~~ değil). Zaman bir geri kayar: Are you certain? → whether he was certain. |
| told me to stop | bırakmamı söylediler | "They told me to stop wasting their time with romantic theories. | **Dolaylı rica/emir:** tell + kişi + to + fiil; olumsuzu not to: had told him twice not to say anything. ~~told me stop~~ değil. |
| she said that | -diğini söyledi | She said that his observation was interesting, but that it did not explain anything. | **Dolaylı anlatım:** say + (that) + cümle; tell + kişi + (that). ~~She said me~~ yanlış: She told me / She said (to me). |
| if I were in their position | onların yerinde olsam | "And honestly, Kofi, if I were in their position, I might do the same. | **2. tip koşul:** If + geçmiş zaman, would/might + fiil. Şimdiki gerçek dışı durum. I, he, she ile de were kullanılır: If I were you, I'd ask… |
| if you had let me | izin verseydiniz | If you had let me test this twenty years ago, you would have known." | **3. tip koşul:** If + had + V3, would have + V3. Olmamış geçmiş: izin vermediler, bilmediler. Kısa biçim: If I'd been more careful… they might have listened. |
| i wish it were | keşke öyle olsaydı | I wish it were." | **wish + geçmiş zaman:** şimdiki duruma dair dilek ve pişmanlık. Türkçedeki "keşke … olsa". ~~I wish it is~~ değil. |
| must have been | olmalıydı (çıkarım) | She must have been about forty when she wrote them, Kofi thought, and very excited. | **Geçmişe dair çıkarım:** must have + V3 = eminim öyleydi; can't have + V3 = olamaz: It can't have been there that morning. might have + V3 = belki. |
| should have told | söylemeliydin | "You should have told me," she said at last. | should have + V3: yapılmamış bir şeye pişmanlık ya da sitem. Olumsuzu: I shouldn't have closed the door on you. |
| they've been learning | öğreniyorlarmış (-dır) | "Then they've been learning the long year," said Yara slowly. | **Present perfect continuous:** have/has been + -ing. Geçmişte başlayıp hâlâ süren eylem: I've been working in the greenhouse for two years. Türkçede şimdiki zaman + -dır: "iki yıldır çalışıyorum" (~~I am working for two years~~ değil). |
| who was nothing if not fair | her şeyden önce adil olan | Councillor Hale, who was nothing if not fair, voted with the others. | **Virgüllü (tanımlamayan) ilgi cümlesi:** kişi zaten belli, ek bilgi verilir; virgülsüz yazılmaz ve that kullanılmaz. Ayrıca: Ms Okafor, who was in charge of the laboratory. |
| i'll be sitting | oturuyor olacağım | "Then I'll be sitting in the front row," she said. | **Future continuous:** will be + -ing = gelecekte belli bir anda sürmekte olacak eylem. Ayrıca: they'll be taking the soil out. |
| you'll have filled | doldurmuş olacaksın | By the time you're my age, you'll have filled a hundred more." | **Future perfect:** will have + V3 = gelecekteki bir ana kadar bitmiş olacak iş; çoğu zaman by the time / by + zaman ile. Ayrıca: By the meeting, it'll have had a week of spring. |
| despite | -e rağmen | Despite her age, she stood perfectly straight. | despite + isim/-ing (~~despite she was old~~ değil). although + cümle: although at the time he did not understand what he was seeing. |
| whereas | oysa, -ken | Instead, the Earth-seed rows would be moved under the lamps for the winter, so that the colony would not go hungry, whereas the Mars-born rows would be allowed to sleep under the real sky until spring. | İki durumu karşılaştırır. however ise cümleler arasında, virgülle kullanılır: The greenhouse computer, however, insisted that everything was normal. |
