# The Window Across
> A1-K05 · Günlük hayat/duygu · H-071 metni (taslak, kullanıcı onayı bekliyor).
> Kurallar: cümle ≤ 8 kelime, present simple, yan cümle yok. İşaretler: docs/KITAPLIK.md §6.2.

## [c1] Cold Mornings

### [p01]
Omar is new in this city. It is a very cold city. There is snow on every street.

Omar is thirty-four. He is from Morocco. He knows no one here.

But every morning at seven, someone waves.

It is a little girl. She lives in the window across the street. She has a yellow hat. She has a big smile.

Omar waves, too. The girl smiles.

But her eyes are not on his face. She always looks down a little.

What does she see?

### [p02]
Omar makes bread. He works in a small bread shop. The shop is under his flat.

Every day, he makes many kinds of bread. He also makes his mother's bread. It is thin and flat. It is the bread of his home.

People come in from the snow. They buy bread. They say, "Thank you." Omar says, "Thank you," too.

That is all. Omar is shy. He does not know many words here.

### [p03]
In the evening, the street is quiet.

Omar eats his dinner alone. He drinks hot green tea. He reads his mother's letters.

His flat is small. It has no fridge.

So Omar puts a cup of milk outside. It stands on the window. It is cold there.

Every evening, the cup is full.

### [p04]
But every morning, the cup is not full.

Omar looks at the cup. He looks at the snow on the window. There are small holes in it. They look like little flowers.

"Is it the wind?" he asks. "Is it a bird?"

He does not know. At seven, he goes to the window.

The girl is there. She smiles at his window. Omar smiles, too. This is the best time of his day.

### [p05]
Omar wants to say hello. He wants to say more.

At night, he practises his words. "Good morning. My name is Omar. Do you like bread?"

But Omar is very, very shy.

One night, there is a lot of snow. The wind is strong. Only the bread shop is warm. It is warm all night.

In the morning, Omar goes to his window. The cup is full. There are no holes in the snow.

The girl is at her window. But she does not wave. She does not smile.

She cries.

## [c2] The Cat

### [p06]
All day, Omar thinks about the girl.

Why does she cry? Is she sick?

The shop is very busy today.

There is a small noise behind the bags. Omar hears it. But he has no time. "It is the wind," he says.

At six o'clock, he closes the shop. He takes some of his mother's bread. He puts on his coat.

Then he stops at the door. His heart is fast.

### [p07]
Omar goes out into the snow. The snow is up to his knees.

He stands in front of the girl's door. He wants to go home. But he thinks of her sad face.

He rings the bell.

A woman opens the door. She has the same yellow hat. The girl is behind her. Her eyes are red.

"Good evening," says Omar. "My name is Omar. I make bread. Are you OK?"

His words are slow. But the woman smiles. "Hello, Omar. I am Anna. This is Freya."

### [p08]
"Do you have a cat?" asks Freya.

"A cat? No," says Omar.

"But she sits on your window. Every morning!" says Freya. "She is grey and white. She drinks your milk. I call her Mimi."

Omar thinks. The milk. The little holes in the snow.

"Every morning, I wave to Mimi," says Freya. "And Mimi looks at me."

So the hello is for a cat. It is not for Omar.

Omar feels a little funny. Then he laughs. Anna laughs, too.

### [p09]
"Today there is no Mimi," says Freya. "There is a lot of snow. Where is she?"

Omar thinks again. The warm shop. The small noise behind the bags.

"Please, come with me," he says.

They go to the bread shop together. The shop is warm and quiet. It smells of bread.

He looks behind the bags of flour.

There is a grey and white cat. She sleeps. Her face is white with flour.

"Mimi!" says Freya.

### [p10]
Mimi opens her eyes. She sees Freya. She goes to Freya and sits with her.

Omar gives Mimi some milk in a bowl. Freya gives her a little bread. Anna makes tea.

"Do you like green tea?" asks Omar.

"Yes, I do," says Anna.

They eat Omar's bread. They talk and laugh. Omar's words are slow. That is OK.

Now every morning, Mimi sits on Omar's window. Freya waves to Mimi. Then she waves to Omar.

And Freya says, "Good morning, Omar!"

Omar has three friends in the cold city. A girl, a mother and a cat.
