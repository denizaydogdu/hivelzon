# Sözlük: The Town That Wasn't There (C2-K02)

İleri kelimeler, deyimler ve eşdizimler; C1/C2 dilbilgisi yapıları (her yapı için metinden en az bir örnek) ve Türk öğrenci notları. Taslak; kullanıcı onayı bekliyor (H-028).

| trap street | tuzak sokak | "It's a trap street," said a voice behind her. | Haritacıların, kopyalayanı yakalamak için haritaya bilerek eklediği var olmayan sokak. Kitapta bütün bir kasabaya genişler: trap town. |
| ford | sığ geçit | Nowhere in the file had anybody mentioned a ford. | Nehrin yürüyerek ya da araçla geçilebilen sığ yeri. Pek çok İngiliz yer adının sonundaki -ford buradan gelir (Callowford). |

Sık çizgi = dik yamaç. |
| hanging valley | asılı vadi | That was the hanging valley on the map, tucked away out of sight. | Ana vadinin yukarısında, bir sırtın arkasında kalan küçük yan vadi. |

| purportedly | güya, iddiaya göre | Instead, she had sent Gavin a message on Sunday night explaining, in her most formal register, that the receipt of correspondence from a purportedly non-existent settlement constituted a material change in the evidence, and that a final site inspection was therefore required before the deletion could responsibly proceed. | Temkin ve mesafe dili (G-C2-03): yazar iddiayı kendisi üstlenmez. |

| arguably | denebilir ki, belki de | It was arguably the most accurate report she had ever written. | Temkin (G-C2-03): iddiayı tartışmaya açık bırakır. "Tartışmasız" anlamına gelmez. |
| so to speak | tabiri caizse | We had a valley and no name, and without a name we were, so to speak, not officially anywhere." | Mecazlı bir ifadeyi yumuşatır. |

| off the beaten track | ücra, yolun dışında | Merrow Cross, if it existed at all, was not so much off the beaten track as off the track altogether. | Deyim: kalabalığın gitmediği yer. Metinde deyimle oynanır: "yolun tamamen dışında". |
| put us on the map | bizi haritaya koymak; bizi ünlü etmek | Every few years somebody offers to put us on the map, as they say. | Deyim: put something on the map = bir yeri tanınır kılmak. Kasaba zaten haritadadır; söz oyunu buradan gelir. |
| wild goose chase | boşuna uğraş, nafile kovalamaca | She was beginning to wonder whether Bryony had sent her on a wild goose chase, perhaps as a hobby, when the trees opened suddenly and the lane ran out onto a wide, flat bank of grey stones. | Deyim: bulunamayacak bir şeyin peşine düşmek. |
| with a pinch of salt | ihtiyatla, temkinle | You could take local legends with a pinch of salt, her father used to say, but you should never throw away the salt. | Deyim: take something with a pinch of salt. Baba deyimi ters çevirerek öğüt verir. |
| go native | yerlilere karışmak | Don't go native." | Deyim: gönderildiği yerin insanlarından biri gibi olmak, tarafsızlığını yitirmek. |
| Never had she seen | Hiç görmemişti | Never had she seen a fake drawn with such care. | Devrik yapı (G-C1-01): olumsuz zarf başa gelince yardımcı fiil özneden önce gelir. **Dikkat:** ~~Never she had seen~~. |
| Hardly had she taken out | … çıkarmıştı ki | Hardly had she taken out her phone to photograph the valley when it buzzed in her hand. | Devrik yapı (G-C1-01): Hardly had … when = … ır … maz. **Dikkat:** when ile kurulur, ~~than~~ değil. |
| Only then did she see | Ancak o zaman gördü | Only then did she see it. | Devrik yapı (G-C1-01): Only then + did + özne + yalın fiil. **Dikkat:** ~~Only then she saw~~. |
| What she needed was | Ona gereken şey … idi | What she needed was one more week with mud on her boots. | Cleft (G-C1-02): vurgulanan bilgi sona taşınır. |
| It was the contour lines, in the end, that bothered her | Onu rahatsız eden şey eş yükselti çizgileriydi | It was the contour lines, in the end, that bothered her. | Cleft (G-C1-02): It was X that … ile X vurgulanır. Türkçede vurgu "… olan şey X idi" ile korunur. |
| If her father hadn't spent | Babası geçirmemiş olsaydı | If her father hadn't spent those winters teaching her to read these lines, she would be lost now, she thought, and probably back in the car. | Karma koşul (G-C1-03): geçmişteki koşul (had + V3) → şimdiki sonuç (would + yalın fiil, now). | Having reached | … e varınca | Having reached the other side, she sat on a rock, emptied her boots, wrung out her socks and put everything back on with the cheerful discomfort of a person who has done something slightly foolish and got away with it. | Ortaç yan cümlesi (G-C1-04): Having + V3 = önce biten eylem. Aynı yapı: "Having spent eleven years…", "left alone in the small room…". |
| needn't have worried | endişelenmesine gerek yokmuş | She needn't have worried. | Modal incelik (G-C1-05): endişelendi ama gerek yoktu. **Dikkat:** didn't need to worry = endişelenmedi, çünkü gerek yoktu; anlam farklıdır. |
| might well have driven past | geçmiş olabilirdiniz (büyük olasılıkla) | You might well have driven past it every day for fifty years without once suspecting that the hill had a pocket. | Modal incelik (G-C1-05): might well = olasılık güçlü. |
| was supposed to be | olması gerekiyordu | She was supposed to be at her new desk. | Modal incelik (G-C1-05): beklenen ama gerçekleşmeyen durum. Ayrıca: "He was supposed to make something nobody would believe." |
| It is said that | Söylendiğine göre | It is said that one nineteenth-century publisher invented an entire lake, and that his rivals reproduced it so faithfully that a fishing club was formed to visit it. | Kişisiz edilgen (G-C1-06). | it's high time somebody | birinin … zamanı geldi de geçiyor | "She says it's high time somebody from the maps came and looked." | Gerçek dışı geçmiş (G-C1-07): it's high time + geçmiş zaman (came), anlam şimdi. **Dikkat:** ~~it's high time somebody comes~~. |
| I'd rather they didn't | … meseler daha iyi olur | "I'd rather they didn't talk about my socks," said Amira. | Gerçek dışı geçmiş (G-C1-07): başkası için would rather + geçmiş zaman. Kendisi için yalın: "I'd rather not tell it for him." |
| as if someone had combed it | sanki biri onu taramıştı | Nothing but trees: a long, unbroken carpet of fir and pine, so even in colour that it looked as if someone had combed it. | Gerçek dışı geçmiş (G-C1-07): as if + past perfect. Bu imge sonda ipucu olarak geri döner. |
| So do I | Ben de (biliyorum) | "So do I," said Amira. | Eksiltme ve yerine koyma (G-C1-08). Benzeri: "Neither did she.", "I did.", "I'd rather not." **Dikkat:** ~~So I do~~ başka anlamdadır. |
| Little did Gavin know | Gavin hiç bilmiyordu | Little did Gavin know how much she wanted to agree with him. | Öne alma (G-C2-01): Little + did + özne + yalın fiil. Ayrıca: "Little did he know that somebody would be." **Dikkat:** ~~Little Gavin knew~~. |
| Such was the stillness of the air that | Hava öylesine durgundu ki | Such was the stillness of the air that Amira could hear, from the green, a dog barking somewhere on the far side of the ridge. | Öne alma (G-C2-01): Such was + isim + that. Benzeri: "So quietly did it appear that…". **Dikkat:** ~~Such the stillness was~~. |
| removal of the feature on the grounds of | … gerekçesiyle nesnenin kaldırılması | Recommendation: removal of the feature on the grounds of persistent non-observation." | Adlaştırma (G-C2-02): fiiller isme dönünce cümlede hiç kimse bir şey yapmaz. | It is essential that each verified settlement be supported | Doğrulanan her yerleşimin desteklenmesi esastır | Section seven, paragraph one, of Gridwell's Verification Standards: "It is essential that each verified settlement be supported by current imagery in which its principal structures are clearly identifiable." | Resmî dilek kipi (G-C2-04): that + özne + yalın fiil (be). **Dikkat:** ~~is supported~~ ya da ~~should supported~~ değil. |
